# VH-148 — The owner's sign-off changes: words, the switch headings, the read line, the trim's cut parts, the select's chevron

## Intent

The owner walked the page and signed VH-124 off on 2026-10-21 with eleven changes, given in chat. Most are words: a browser sentence finished, "closing graphics" for a bare "closing", line breaks where two sentences run together, a smaller output's uses naming Moodle, the statement's line about who runs the tool removed. Three are design: the switch headings carry too little weight to lead their groups, the trim bar's cut parts read like the part kept, and a select's chevron sits against its right edge. One is about a signal: the read line's duration and size repeat Video properties, while "no sound" can be an error the owner wants seen.

## Acceptance criteria

- `browser.passed` reads "This app is designed and built for Chrome, and this browser has passed the checks for it to run."
- The switch headings (Video type, Video language, and Page language with them) are larger or bolder than their segments and helper, from the type tokens on the Carbon scale, so each reads as the heading of its group; the switchers still move nothing on a choice (VH-135).
- Wherever the page names the closing on its own as a noun, it says "closing graphics", the verb agreeing; "closing card" and the legend "Closing branding" stay; every changed string listed with the change.
- Video language's helper puts "What you type is kept as typed." on a line of its own; in step 3 (title card and closing), a sentence that states a separate fact, such as "The card says “Internal use only” under the logo.", starts a new line where that reads more easily.
- The read line drops the duration and the frame size; with sound it says only that the video was read; with no sound it keeps the words and shows them bold, and a screen reader still hears them.
- In the trim bar, the parts outside the handles are drawn apart from the part kept (lighter, hatched or coloured), with 3:1 against what is next to them, and still apart in the dark theme and forced colours.
- A select's chevron has room on its right, from a spacing token.
- The smaller output's helper names Moodle assignment uploads beside messaging and email.
- The accessibility statement no longer says "Joe Bell runs it at the University of Nottingham."
- The footer's help sends readers to the Creators team on Teams, in a new tab and saying so, and to the learning content team by email at its own address, both from `src/config/`; the feedback dialog and the statement's contact keep the owner's address.
- The design pass's three readings for the walk-through, taken as the agent recommends: a disabled button takes the dashed edge a disabled field takes; the footer's panel headings hold the 16 px subheading step; the footer's two contact links take no full stop, being labels, named as an exception where VH-124's rule is pinned.
- Every changed string is in all three language tables through the strings sheet, the Chinese and Malay marked as the agent's drafts; `test/screen-text.test.ts` and other pins updated, none weakened; tests by category; `npm run check` green with no new skip; checked in Chrome at 1280 px and 375 px in all three languages, light, dark and forced colours.

# VH-166 — One type set: eight named roles, each with one look, and every heading of a level the same

## Intent

The page draws its text in ten authored styles where it has eight jobs. A second-level heading is Lora 20 px in the steps and Arial 16 px in the footer; the step heading takes the body's line height; the statement's subheading differs from its own heading only by a tighter line, since Arial, where it draws, has one Bold for 600 and 700; and the switchers' legends take a third look for a section-level title (V-32). This item names eight roles, gives each one size, weight and line height from the tokens that exist, and groups the selectors that wear each, so the owner sees one type set. On the owner's answer it also gives the footer's two headings the steps' panel heading look, sets the footer's panels at the steps' 24 px (V-37) and the header band's phone inset at the main column's (V-36).

## Acceptance criteria

- V-32: eight roles, each one rule in `app.css` grouping its selectors: page title, panel heading, section heading, body, supporting, compact emphasis, status mark, code, with the looks in the design pass's table; every panel heading 20/28, Carbon's `heading-03` line; the switchers' legends, notification titles and the statement's `h3` one section-heading look.
- V-32, the owner's answer: the footer's two `h2`s take the panel heading look, Lora 20, as the steps' headings do.
- `UI-STANDARDS.md` names the eight roles and their Carbon references as local adaptations, within its 2,400 words or with the owner asked to raise it.
- V-32's role table: links inherit their text's role; text fields and textareas stay at 16 px; `strong` in compact text takes compact emphasis; the selected segment's reserved width kept; tracking `normal` stated once; a fallback face checked.
- V-37, the footer's panels at `.panel`'s 24 px (None's 16 px and the summary's padding stay); V-36, the header band's inset at `--spacing-06` below the width where the column meets its gutters, the desktop logo at the window's edge, the brand rule's exception recorded in a decision and UI-STANDARDS.
- The inventory taken again (`inventory.mjs`) beside the before: one row per role in §1 and §1.headings; the before captures taken again side by side; three languages, light, dark and forced colours; WCAG re-checked for what changed (1.4.6, 1.4.12, 2.4.13).
- `test/stylesheet.test.ts` pins the roles; `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped; our own CSS on the two token files only; no control or element added; the progress bar kept.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Codex's blind review is saved first.** No finding enters the checkout before it is.
- **The journeys.** J1 to J4 each judged as a whole: action prominence, decision burden, reading order, repetition and continuity; page height per state against Phase 7's after-run.
- **Visual design, Carbon productive.** Hierarchy, rhythm, alignment and density per scenario; every text element on one of the eight roles; running text within the 28rem measure; one look per component kind in every state; dark and forced colours as designed states.
- **UX, Nielsen's ten.** One row per heuristic with a verdict and evidence; the conveyor's flow; prevention and recovery per failure and warning, the limiting warning among them; the wording against UI-STANDARDS and the approved Phase 8 sheet, one name per thing across the page and three languages.
- **WCAG 2.2 AAA.** The 86 criteria, each with applicability, scoped evidence, a verdict, any unresolved part and Phase 7's verdict beside it; each gap typed as a defect, an accepted limitation or missing evidence; 1.4.8's five parts; raw mixed-script line counts with the reading stated.
- **Draft findings** from V-48 in the design-pass-2 report's format, each naming its heuristic, criterion or Carbon rule; no duplicate of V-01 to V-47, and a regression reported under its old ID. A direction that would add a control, checkbox or element is marked for the owner and recommended against.
- **The person checklist** revised for what Phase 8 changed, in plain words, with the build link, test clips, tasks and expected outcomes, a report form and the feedback address from `src/config/feedback.ts`.
- No product code changes; the gate and the checker green.

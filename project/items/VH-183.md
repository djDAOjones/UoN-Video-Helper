# VH-183 — The keyboard trim's Page step scales with long videos

## Intent

On the trim track, Page Up and Page Down move a handle by a fixed 10 seconds, so on an hour-long recording reaching the far end by keyboard takes hundreds of presses. This item makes the step the larger of 10 seconds and 1% of the video's length, so keyboard users can move along a long video. It needs no new words and no new control, so it can start before the wording sheet is approved (PLAN-8).

## Acceptance criteria

- Page Up and Page Down move a handle by max(10 s, 1% of the duration), from `src/config/trim.ts`, with no literal elsewhere; the arrow keys and Home and End keep their present steps.
- `trimKeyTarget` takes the duration it needs, and `src/ui/trim.test.ts` covers a short video (10 s step), an hour (36 s step) and the clamp at both ends.
- What the handle says to a screen reader is unchanged.
- Checked in Chrome at 1280, 375 and 320 px, in light, dark and forced colours, and in three languages; `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped; the digest rule applied named at close (rule 8); Codex astra checks the candidate diff before commit, and the commit carries its `Checked:` line.

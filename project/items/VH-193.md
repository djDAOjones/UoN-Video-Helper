# VH-193 — A warning after the job when levelling needed heavy limiting

## Intent

Landing a quiet recording with loud peaks on −16 LUFS can need the limiter to take off a great deal: about 8 LU on the 29-minute Teams recording (VH-106). That can make a voice sound flat, and today the user finds out only by listening to the result. This item adds an advisory warning with the finished video, through the pipeline's `outputWarnings` (`src/media/pipeline.ts`), the worker and the job's result, as the carried-metadata warning is, when the job's measured limiter bite is over 3 LU (round 2's Q6, option 1), so heavy processing is disclosed before the person saves, and VH-192's player lets them listen first (INTAKE.3). The words are row B11 of the wording sheet; spec §5.4 gains a row by correction S25, and its introduction names the after-job rows by S32.

## Acceptance criteria

- The bite is measured in the job, not predicted: `afterChainLufs` + the solved gain − `limitedLufs`, carried out of `AudioPlan` unrounded and before the codec (the log's rounded figures are for reading). The warning shows when it is over `HEAVY_LIMITING_LU` = 3 in `src/config/audio.ts`; it is pushed onto `outputWarnings` in `src/media/pipeline.ts` and travels through `src/workers/job.worker.ts` and `protocol.ts` to the result and its announcement in `src/main.ts`, as the carried-metadata warning does. `detectOutputWarning` has no production caller and is not the path. There is no pre-flight row, and the output loudness check is unchanged.
- On the Teams recording the warning shows, and the bite it acts on is the one the job logged (about 8 LU). Every other corpus file in `samples/` is listed in the close with its bite and whether it warned; a warning on a typical lecture stops the item and goes to the owner.
- The warning reads as the sheet's B11 approves (heading "The loudest moments were held down hard", its detail ending "Next time, try adjusting how you record the sound."), is advisory and blocks nothing, and is shown and spoken with the finished video as the carried-metadata warning is.
- Spec §5.4 reads as corrections S25 and S32 approve, applied in this item's commit, within the spec's 8,100 words; the EBU harness passes unchanged.
- The English is the sheet's, as the owner approved it; the Chinese and Malay rows are the agent's machine drafts, through the filled sheet and `scripts/strings-table.mjs`, marked so for a native reader; `src/i18n/tables.test.ts` is green, and any key removed goes from all three tables and `src/i18n/sheet.ts` in the same commit.
- Checked in Chrome at 1280, 375 and 320 px, in light, dark and forced colours, and in three languages; `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped; the digest rule applied named at close (rule 8); Codex astra checks the candidate diff before commit, and the commit carries its `Checked:` line.

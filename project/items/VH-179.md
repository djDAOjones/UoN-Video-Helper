# VH-179 — Three focus rings drawn whole, and the feedback message held to the measure

## Intent

The second design pass measured three focus rings that fail WCAG 2.4.13. The file button's ring is clipped to a sliver in every state, and under forced colours it keeps a brand colour (V-40). At a 32 px default font the trim handles' ring sits on their own dark disc at 2.17:1 (V-41). Under a light contrast theme the language switcher's ring is white on its white neighbour (V-42). The same pass found the feedback dialog's message running past the house measure, which the card fields keep (V-33, an incomplete fix of VH-167). All four are our own CSS, in `src/styles/app.css` with its tokens and `test/stylesheet.test.ts`, so one item takes them, and one Codex check reads them together. No element is added and the chooser stays native (VH-168). It comes after VH-178, which changes behaviour only, and before VH-180, so the words are measured on the final CSS.

## Acceptance criteria

- **V-40, the ring's size.** `.file-input` gives its button room of the ring's width plus its offset (`--focus-width` + `--focus-offset`) inside its own box, as VH-171's passing twin did. The room is offset by an equal negative margin if the drop zone's padding (`--spacing-05`) allows, so the button does not move and the step is no taller. Focused, the ring is a whole 2 px perimeter at 3:1 against its unfocused state. That holds chosen and unchosen, keyboard-pressed, at the default and the 32 px font, in all 110 records `digest-focus-2026-10-23.md` failed (88 px² changed before, against 640 needed).
- **V-40, the ring's colour.** Under forced colours, the unchosen button's ring (opted out, painted Highlight) takes `CanvasText`, as the primary `.button`'s does (`app.css:1675`). The chosen button's ring is read and given a system colour if it keeps a token. Both are read in the light and dark emulated palettes, focused and pressed. Before, VH-174's live reading: `rgb(16, 38, 59)` in light and white in dark.
- **V-41.** The handles' disc stops are sized from the thumb, not the root font. The stops come from px tokens beside `--target-min` in `tokens.carbon.css`, never a literal in `app.css`, so the clear band under the ring keeps its width at any font size. That covers the WebKit thumb, the Firefox thumb, the forced-colours thumbs and the disabled thumb (`:1351`–`1364`, `:1738`–`1745`, and the rule after them). At the 32 px font both handles' rings pass against the painted disc (0 px² before, against 264 and 270, in `S-15__{en-GB,zh-Hans}__w1280__font32`). The default size does not regress (246–256 px² against 157, 288 under forced-dark).
- **V-42.** Under forced colours the band segment's ring is drawn inside the focused face, clear of its neighbours, in a system colour that contrasts with the face it lies on. That is `HighlightText` on the chosen face's `Highlight`, or another colour shown to pass. It must not be white on the dark palette's cyan Highlight. It is read in both forced palettes in `S-03__{en-GB,zh-Hans,ms-MY}__w1280` (416–480 px² against 498–520 in forced-light before). The ring outside forced colours is unchanged.
- **V-33.** The feedback message (`#feedback-message`, a `.textarea`) takes `max-width: var(--measure)` as the card fields do, a cap and never a width, so it yields to a narrower dialog. Drawn lines are within 448 px at 1280 px in three languages (541, 541 and 524 px before, in `S-79a` and `S-79b`), within 896 px at the 32 px font, and with the fallback faces. The dialog and its buttons stay as they are. The `test/stylesheet.test.ts` selector list (`:343`) grows with it.
- **V-44, the owner's added criterion (VH-174.1).** Under both forced palettes, a filled button (the primary `.button`) and a danger button show a change on hover: the border alone changes, to a second system colour that contrasts with the face and with the border at rest. The fill, the text and VH-169's inverted press stay as they are, and the press still wins over hover. The unchosen file button's hover is read with them and given the same rule if it shows no change. Read hovered, at rest and pressed in the light and dark emulated palettes, in three languages. Outside forced colours nothing changes. `test/stylesheet.test.ts` pins the rule. The commit's Checked scope adds V-44.
- **V-18 not worsened.** The painted file button's bounds (the button, not the input box the room enlarges) are read in J1's first screen at 1280 × 900 and 320 × 568, in three languages, beside V-18's readings (844–888 px in English at 1280). In none of the six accepted cases does the button sit lower than before, and in English at 1280 it stays wholly inside the first screen. If any case is worse, the item stops and the owner is asked, since V-18 stands accepted as it is. VH-180 and VH-176 repeat the reading on their final words.
- **VH-178's blocking-check hand-off, re-walked.** With focus on `#trim-start` when `prores-1080p.mov` blocks, focus lands on the file input and its ring now passes 2.4.13, in light, dark and both forced palettes.
- **The measure's rule, recorded.** UI-STANDARDS' measure line (`UI-STANDARDS.md`, Perceivable) states the owner's ruling for a line that mixes Chinese and Latin: a CJK glyph counted as two characters against 80. WCAG 1.4.8 stays met by G204's reflow, kept apart from the house count. The rule stays within the 2,400 words by trading words elsewhere, or the owner is asked.
- **Measured, before and after, with the pass's walker:** the focus instrument on the records above, and the measure, overflow and target instruments on every record the change touches. All of it in three languages and light, dark, forced-light and forced-dark, with captures of each ring beside the before. The table is kept in the bundle's measurements folder as vh179-rings-2026-10-23.md. `test/stylesheet.test.ts` pins the ring's room, the forced-colour ring rules and the disc tokens.
- **VH-176's stage rule, kept:** before and after measured with the pass's walker in three languages and light, dark and forced colours, with WCAG re-checked for what it touched (2.4.7, 2.4.11, 2.4.13, 1.4.11, 1.4.4, 1.4.8, 1.4.10, 2.5.5); our own CSS on the two token files; no control, checkbox or element added; the progress bar kept. UI-STANDARDS updated only where a rule changes, within its 2,400 words (it stands at 2,400), or with the owner asked. A change to `docs/` is drafted as a wish line for the owner. `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped, and the digest rules applied named (rule 8).

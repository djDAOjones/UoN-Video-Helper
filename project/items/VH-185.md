# VH-185 — A sliver of late sound in the kept part is caught before Create, not after the encode

## Intent

When the part kept ends just after a late sound begins, it holds a sliver of that sound, such as 22 ms of AAC priming for a sound starting at 4 s with the video kept to 0:04. Pre-flight lets the job start, and it fails at the output check ("invalid-measurement") after the whole encode. This item treats a kept sound shorter than the meter's 400 ms block as no sound to level, so the job finishes and the existing no-audio wording says so before Create. It needs no new words, so it can start before the wording sheet is approved (PLAN-8).

## Acceptance criteria

- A kept sound shorter than one 400 ms gating block is treated by pre-flight as no sound to level; the job runs and finishes, and the output check does not fail on it. The block length is the meter's own `BLOCK_SECONDS` in `src/audio/loudness.ts`, never a second literal; that file is protected, so exporting it re-runs the EBU harness.
- Before Create, the existing no-audio warning ("This video has no audio") shows, as for a file with no sound in the part kept. If the build finds that sentence untrue for this case, it stops and raises a wish line for the owner's words rather than writing new ones.
- A test reproduces VH-139's case (a sound starting at 4 s, kept to 0:04) and fails before the fix; a kept sound of 400 ms or more still levels as before; the EBU harness passes unchanged.
- Nothing silently lost: what the output carries for that sliver (silence or nothing) is stated in the close.
- Checked in Chrome at 1280, 375 and 320 px, in light, dark and forced colours, and in three languages; `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped; the digest rule applied named at close (rule 8); Codex astra checks the candidate diff before commit, and the commit carries its `Checked:` line.

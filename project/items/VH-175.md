## Acceptance criteria

- **Planned with `verbs/plan.md`.** Every rank 1 and rank 2 finding sits in a fix item in Phase 7, between VH-175 and VH-176, each with a file to the schema whose criteria carry VH-176's stage rule; each item one session's work; an order with its reason; short backlog lines within the 1,000-word budget.
- **Rank 3** findings go to the owner as proposals; any not taken become wish lines.
- **Codex astra's critique of the fix plan**, from a brief, saved verbatim in the bundle; each point applied, refused with a reason, or for the owner.

- A rank 1 finding may be fixed at once on the owner's word, as VH-164 was.
- A PLAN-7 entry whose Items line names the fix items; `node tools/view.mjs` run; the gate and the checker green; the commit carries `Checked: gpt-6-astra — <verdict> — the fix plan`.

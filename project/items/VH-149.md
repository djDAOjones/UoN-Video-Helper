# VH-149 — The review of Codex's 21 October run: step 1's line keeps its words, the closing graphics' translations read again, a pin restored

## Intent

Codex built VH-148, Phase 3 and Phase 4 alone on 21 October, pushed each to `main`, and no second model checked them. Claude's review of 22 October found five rank-1 defects, live on the pilot. Two VH-141 announcements were written over step 1's status line: a blocked start-up lost its reason and remedy, and a refused preview lost "Video read." and its bold "No sound.". A VH-141 test lost half its pin. VH-148's word-for-word "closing graphics" broke a Chinese sentence and five Malay ones, and rewrote the statement's kept sentence in two languages. This item fixes those five and nothing else; the review's rank-2 and rank-3 findings go to the owner.

## Acceptance criteria

- Errors captured appearing, and a preview the player refuses, are each said once through a visually hidden polite live region on the page in every state, outside every step and the errors panel; step 1's status line keeps what it said — a start-up block's reason, "Video read." and its bold "No sound."; the region is emptied on a change of language.
- The save line's pin again says `saveFailed` never goes through `setStatus`.
- Chinese `trim.intro` says the start or the end of the video, not its closing graphics; the five Malay sentences after a full stop begin with a capital; the Chinese and Malay `statement.covers` read as they did before VH-148, less the dropped sentence; all through the strings sheet.
- Regression tests by category for each; `npm run check` green with no new skip; checked in Chrome at 1280 and 375 px, with the before state reproduced on the live pilot.

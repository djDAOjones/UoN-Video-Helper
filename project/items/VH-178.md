# VH-178 — Focus passes on when finished work takes its control away

## Intent

The second design pass found three transitions that remove the control holding focus and drop DOM focus to the page's body: a job ending with focus on Announce progress, a blocking check with focus in a setup field, and a check settling during a save with focus on Stop the check (V-46, WCAG 2.4.3). DOM focus on the body is what was read; where the next Tab lands and what a screen reader then says were not measured. This item extends the existing hand-off, `focusHeldBy` in `src/main.ts`, so each caller names every control it takes away and the helper never focuses a successor that cannot take focus. It adds no element, and it changes no successor a caller chooses today when that successor can take focus. It goes first in the fix order: it is the pass's one failure at level A, and the only change to behaviour, in `main.ts` and the walker's scenarios.

## Acceptance criteria

- **V-46, the job's end.** Both endings (`src/main.ts`, `:2947` and `:3008`) capture Announce progress (`announceField` and its checkbox) with Cancel. With focus on Announce progress when a job is made, cancelled or fails, focus lands on the successor the ending chooses today: the result, the failure's explanation or Create.
- **V-46, the blocking check.** Only the blocking branch that withdraws steps 2 to 5 (`:1580`, `:1592`) captures their focusable controls with Stop the check. The shared capture at `:1535` stays Stop the check alone, so a ready or discouraging verdict (`:1614`) and a storage block the setup steps resolve (`setupStepsResolve`) leave focus in the setup control that survives. With focus on `#trim-start` when `prores-1080p.mov` blocks, focus lands on the file input.
- **V-46, the check that settles during a save.** The helper focuses its target only if it can take focus: connected, not hidden, not disabled, and visible (`checkVisibility()`, or the same test). A box made focusable with `tabIndex = -1` counts. When the target cannot take focus, the helper falls back to the next visible, enabled control that continues the task, which is Stop saving while a save runs. The fallback is the caller's ordered choice or one rule in the helper, never a new element. In S-65, with the save slowed by F4b and focus on Stop the check, focus lands on Stop saving.
- **The walker's transition variants**, declared in its scenarios and kept for VH-176. (a) S-65 with the save slowed to 3 s a chunk (an F4b variant), asserting the save is still running when the check settles and reading focus before any instrument moves it. (b) Announce progress held through a job made, cancelled, failed by reply and failed by the watchdog (`:3008`–`3050`, where the hand-off waits on `settled(id)`). (c) A reader moving focus elsewhere between the capture and the hand-off, in the watchdog branch and in a save. (d) The feedback dialog open at the transition. (e) A ready verdict and a recoverable storage block with focus in a setup field, which keeps it.
- **The guards kept.** Focus is never taken from the feedback dialog, nor from where a person has gone to read (S-72, `main.ts:898`'s "still lost" test). Every other caller (`:1633`, `:1980`, `:2786`, `:2845`, `:3247`, `:3271`, `:3286`, `:3362`) keeps its behaviour, checked by reading each one and by the keyboard routes.
- **Measured, before and after, with the pass's walker.** Each of the three cases is walked by keys in English at 1280 px in light. Its after-state, the focused element and the ring's pixels on it (2.4.13: 3:1, a 2 px perimeter), is read in zh-Hans and ms-MY, and in dark, forced-light and forced-dark. Where the successor is the file input, its ring is V-40's: recorded as still failing and left to VH-179, which re-walks this hand-off. VH-178's own criterion is the successor reached. The before is VH-173's J1 `focusAfterEnd: body` and the scratchpad cases `d01b` and `d01c2`, which give the body. The eight keyboard routes pass as before (238 of 242, the other four the native popup). The table is kept in the bundle's measurements folder as vh178-focus-2026-10-23.md.
- **A unit test pins the fallback** if the helper can be moved out of `main.ts` into a module tested as the `src/ui` tests are, with no change of behaviour; if not, the walker's cases are its proof, and the close says so.
- **VH-176's stage rule, kept:** before and after measured with the pass's walker in three languages and light, dark and forced colours, with WCAG re-checked for what it touched (2.4.3, 2.4.7, 2.4.11, 2.4.13); our own code on the two token files; no control, checkbox or element added; the progress bar kept. UI-STANDARDS updated only where a rule changes, within its 2,400 words (it stands at 2,400), or with the owner asked. A change to `docs/` is drafted as a wish line for the owner. `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped, and the digest rules applied named (rule 8). Codex astra checks the final candidate diff with its base and evidence, from a brief, before commit; a failing point is repaired and re-checked with the follow-up verdict kept, or, if Claude disagrees, the item stays open and the point goes to the owner. The commit carries `Checked: gpt-6-astra — <verdict as given> — V-46`.

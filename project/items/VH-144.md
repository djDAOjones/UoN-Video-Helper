# VH-144 — The closing's reason said once under None; the Malay time fields keep their width at 320 px

## Intent

Two defects the design pass of 21 October found on the live page, each a breach of a rule VH-124 or the profile set. Under None, step 4 says "Not used with None." three times, once under each locked control (V-20). In Malay at 320 px, step 2's time fields shrink to 26 px wide beside their long "Set … here" buttons, under the 44 px target and too narrow to show a time (V-23). Both are small and seen by every user who reaches them; this item fixes them first in Phase 4.

## Acceptance criteria

- V-20: under None, one line beneath the three locked controls (Animation onset, Colour, Internal use only) says why, once; each control keeps the reason in its `aria-describedby`; under Cut, onset's own reason stands; the result line is unchanged; in all three languages with no new string unless one is needed, through the strings sheet if so.
- V-23: step 2's time field never falls under `--field-min-width`: where the row cannot hold the field at that width beside its button, the button goes under the field — scoped to the two trim rows, since `.control-with-help` also holds the onset select and its help button; checked at 320 px and at the 640 px stand-in for 200% in Malay, Chinese and English, on both sides of the switch and while locked; no target under 44 px in the walker's digests.
- No new token; tests by category (a closing-choice test for the one reason; a stylesheet or layout pin for the wrap where one can be written); `npm run check` green with no new skip; checked in Chrome with the walker.

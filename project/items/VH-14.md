# VH-14 — Deployment

## Intent

Pages is viable — no COOP/COEP needed, and asset URLs derive from `import.meta.env.BASE_URL`. What is unsettled is whether it should stay there: a Pages site on a personal account is public and serves UoN branding from `djdaojones.github.io`. Public hosting was accepted for an unadvertised pilot; the intended home is an internal server. **Every push to `main` deploys** — there is no separate act of publishing. VH-65 hardens that boundary.

- The owner's part is named on the backlog line's blocker; nothing closes without it.

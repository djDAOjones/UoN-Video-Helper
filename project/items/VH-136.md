# VH-136 — A time left during the job

## Intent

The page estimates a job's time only before it starts. Once it is running, a member of staff has a percentage and a stage, but no idea how long is left. VH-116 measured that the job's own encoding rate, read after 10 s of encoding, predicted the real time within 4% on every run long enough to reach it. The owner asked on 2026-10-20 for a time left during the job. Where and how it shows is the owner's to weigh against the brief's conveyor, and against VH-109's rule that progress neither freezes nor chatters.

## Acceptance criteria

- After the job has encoded for a set time (10 s, measured by VH-116, the number in `src/config/`), the page shows the time left, from the job's own encoding rate, where the owner chooses; before that, nothing new shows.
- The time left updates without chattering: a screen reader hears it no more often than the owner chooses, never on every update.
- Its words are in all three language tables; the English passes the readability gate.
- The spec says so: a drafted edit for the owner's approval, applied word for word.
- Tests by category; `npm run check` green with no new skip; checked in Chrome with real encodes of at least two samples, the shown time against the real one.

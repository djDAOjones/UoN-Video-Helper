## Intent

## Acceptance criteria

- **The stage rule, kept by every fix item:**
  - its before and after measured with the pass's walker, in three languages and light, dark and forced colours, with WCAG re-checked for what it touched;
  - our own CSS on the two token files; no control, checkbox or element added; the progress bar kept;
  - UI-STANDARDS updated where a rule changes, within its 2,400 words or with the owner asked; a change to `docs/` drafted as a wish line for the owner;
  - the gate green with no test deleted or skipped, and the digest rule it applied named (rule 8);
  - Codex astra's check before commit, of the final candidate diff with its base and evidence, from a brief; a failing point repaired and re-checked, with the follow-up verdict kept, or, if Claude disagrees, the item left open and the point put to the owner; the commit carries `Checked: gpt-6-astra — <verdict as given> — <scope>`.
- **Each fix linked** to its finding and its verification in the report's Disposition table.
- **The after-run.** VH-199's matrix walked again with VH-198's instruments, beside VH-199's before, through the manifest, every changed measurement explained; the Phase 8 contract and the new fixes re-verified. With no fix items, the after-run still runs, over the cells any later commit touched.
- **The format "?", for the owner.** The after-run captures VH-205's format "?" beside the lede's fourth promise in its states (at rest, hovered, focused and open), in en-GB, zh-Hans and ms-MY, in light, dark, forced light and forced dark, and at the 32 px font, so that the owner sees it looks right, not only that it measures right ("we also need to make sure that question mark about file formats looks good", 2026-10-27). The owner approved two changes to it, which VH-211 plans; the captures are of the "?" after them. The captures go to the owner; a change the owner asks for becomes a fix item, planned the same way, before this item closes.
- **The bar.** The owner, 2026-10-27, in the Backlog review chat: "ensure everything passes UX / design / Accessibility". That chat read it as the bar for this item's integrated check, and it is recorded as that chat's reading: the after-run and Codex's integrated check judge Phase 8 against the pass's UX, design and accessibility lenses (the journeys and Nielsen, Carbon and the tokens, WCAG 2.2 AAA), and a failure is fixed or put to the owner.
- **Codex astra's integrated check** of the diff from Phase 8's start (the VH-177 close) against the after-run, saved verbatim; each point applied, refused with a reason, or for the owner.
- Closes when the after-run is accounted for and every review point is applied, refused or decided by the owner, not merely when the fixes have shipped; the gate and the checker green.

# VH-146 — The Submission card keeps its date and its note whole

## Intent

All 37 frames the cards spike drew on 21 October pass the owner's 28 pt floor at every shape in every language. At the worst case, every field at its limit, the design pass found the type falling short in two ways (V-30): the card date's note can break inside itself — in Chinese at 4:3, "（非 / 提交日期）", leaving "not" at one line's end and "submission date" at the next line's start, the misreading the note exists to stop — and a date can break between its day and month. Nothing is lost and a reader who reads on gets it, but a phrase the app writes itself should not split. This item keeps Submission's generated date and its note whole, with the spike proving the worst cases still fit.

## Acceptance criteria

- The renderer draws Submission's generated card date, and its note ("— not the submission date" in English and Malay, the bracketed form in Chinese), each as one unbreakable piece: a line may break before or after it, never inside it, in all three languages. A date typed on the staff card keeps its ordinary breaks.
- `spike-cards.html` re-run with the walker's `cards` scenario: ALL PASS, every size logged, the clips kept beside the item's close.
- Tests by category in `title-card.test.ts` (an unbreakable date, an unbreakable note, a typed date still breaking); `npm run check` green with no new skip; the card's words unchanged.

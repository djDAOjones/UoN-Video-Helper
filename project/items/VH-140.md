# VH-140 — The record names the card and the words' language; the switches say which job they change; Step 3 says its rules first

## Intent

V-02, V-05 and V-06 of the 20 October audit. The one-line record a result and its discard question carry cannot tell a staff card from a submission card, nor say the language the app drew its words in, so two outputs of the same recording read the same — the rank-1 case A-05 named. The two video switches stay live during a job, as the spec wants, but nothing says a change is for the next video or shows the running job's own type and language. Step 3 states its rules only by refusing: four errors at once for a submission, no word before Create that all four fields are needed, nothing under the staff box about what makes a card.

## Acceptance criteria

- The record says "a 5-second staff title card" or "a 5-second submission card" and, when the words' language differs from the page's, names it; never the card's words; the discard question carries the same; the "Previous video" naming is unchanged.
- While a job runs or a result stands, one line under Video type and Video language says a change applies to the next video made, and the running job's own type and language are shown with its progress; Page language is not touched.
- Under Student submission a line says all four fields are needed, with `aria-required` on them; under Staff video a line says any of the four fields makes a card and that the date is filled for you; the per-field errors stay as they are.
- Every new string is in all three language tables through the strings sheet; the Chinese and Malay are marked as the agent's drafts for the colleagues.
- Tests by category; `npm run check` green with no new skip; checked in Chrome at 1280 px and 375 px in all three languages, on both sides of the switch, with a job running.
- V-29 from the design pass (2026-10-21): Submission's ID length and most IDs reach its words from config, as the dated note of 2026-10-21 says.

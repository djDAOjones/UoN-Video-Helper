# VH-176 — The second pass's integrated verification: the stage rule, the after-run, Codex astra's integrated check

## Intent

PLAN-7's fix stage. The fix items VH-175 plans own their own builds; this item holds the rule each of them keeps, so every fix is built and checked one way, with Codex astra checking each before it closes. ## Acceptance criteria

- **The stage rule, kept by every fix item:**
  - its before and after measured with the pass's walker, in three languages and light, dark and forced colours, with WCAG re-checked for what it touched;
  - our own CSS on the two token files; no control, checkbox or element added; the progress bar kept;
  - UI-STANDARDS updated where a rule changes, within its 2,400 words or with the owner asked; a change to `docs/` drafted as a wish line for the owner;
  - the gate green with no test deleted or skipped, and the digest rule it applied named (rule 8);
  - Codex astra's check before commit, of the final candidate diff with its base and evidence, from a brief; a failing point repaired and re-checked, with the follow-up verdict kept, or, if Claude disagrees, the item left open and the point put to the owner; the commit carries `Checked: gpt-6-astra — <verdict as given> — <scope>`.
- **Each fix linked** to its finding and its verification in the report's Disposition table.
- **The after-run.** The full PLAN-7 matrix walked again with VH-171's instruments, beside VH-172's before, through the manifest, every changed measurement explained; the six-fix contract and the new fixes re-verified.
- **Codex astra's integrated check** of the diff from the pass's start against the after-run, saved verbatim; each point applied, refused with a reason, or for the owner.
- Closes when the after-run is accounted for and every review point is applied, refused or decided by the owner, not merely when the fixes have shipped; the gate and the checker green.

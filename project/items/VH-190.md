# VH-190 — "Create the video" under a finished video says it makes the video again

## Intent

With a finished video on screen, the secondary "Create the video" button stays under the card with nothing to say that it makes the video again with the current settings, and discards the unsaved one. This item gives the button a label for that state (VH-182.7). The question before an unsaved video is discarded already exists, so no new element is added. The words are row B8 of the wording sheet.

## Acceptance criteria

- While a finished video is on screen, the button reads as the sheet's B8 approves; at every other time it reads "Create the video". Its accessible name follows its label.
- Pressing it with the video unsaved still asks the existing discard question first; nothing else about the flow changes.
- Focus is not moved by the relabel, and a screen reader hears the new name only when it reaches the button.
- The English is the sheet's, as the owner approved it; the Chinese and Malay rows are the agent's machine drafts, through the filled sheet and `scripts/strings-table.mjs`, marked so for a native reader; `src/i18n/tables.test.ts` is green, and any key removed goes from all three tables and `src/i18n/sheet.ts` in the same commit.
- Checked in Chrome at 1280, 375 and 320 px, in light, dark and forced colours, and in three languages; `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped; the digest rule applied named at close (rule 8); Codex astra checks the candidate diff before commit, and the commit carries its `Checked:` line.

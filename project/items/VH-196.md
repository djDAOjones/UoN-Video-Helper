# VH-196 — The lede's five promises in the owner's words, with an MP4 hint beside "Saves in a reliable format."

## Intent

The lede promises three things and leaves out trimming, so a novice is surprised by step 2. The owner noticed this: "the intro text mentions all the features except for the trimming - users, especially novices, will be suprised by it". He also wanted the format and the size said apart, for a novice. The lede's list becomes his five promises, and a "?" toggletip beside promise 4 explains why the format matters. The toggletip reuses VH-184's onset toggletip pattern. It is a control the owner asked for himself, so it is his exception to Phase 8's no-new-control rule.

## Acceptance criteria

- The lede's list reads, in order, as the owner set it: "Lets you trim unwanted footage from the start and end.", "Adds opening and closing graphics.", "Evens out the audio level for better audience experience.", "Saves in a reliable format." and "Makes the file the right size for your purpose." Promises 1 and 3 replace VH-187's shipped promises 1 to 3.
- Beside promise 4 is a "?" toggletip, the same pattern as the onset toggletip: a button that discloses by click, Enter or Space, never by hover alone, with a name that says what it is about, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and Escape and focus-moving-on closing it as VH-184 settled. Its text is "Videos come in many formats. Some play on one device but not another, or do not work with some apps. This tool saves your video as an MP4, the most interoperable video format. That means it plays on almost any phone, computer or website."
- Every new English string passes the readability gate, as the owner's words do (graded 2.5, under 8 words, 8.9, under 8 words and 2.3; the hint 5.3); "MP4" passes the abbreviation check.
- The file button's position is measured before and after, as VH-187 measured it, since the lede grows and gains a Tab stop; nothing runs off the page at 320 px.
- The Chinese and Malay rows are the agent's machine drafts, through the filled sheet and `scripts/strings-table.mjs`, marked so for a native reader; `src/i18n/tables.test.ts` is green.
- Checked in Chrome at 1280, 375 and 320 px, in light, dark and forced colours, and in three languages; `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped; the digest rule applied named at close (rule 8); Codex astra checks the candidate diff before commit, and the commit carries its `Checked:` line.

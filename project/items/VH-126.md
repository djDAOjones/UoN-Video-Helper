# VH-126 — The Internal only closing

## Intent

The owner asked on 2026-10-14, and settled on 2026-10-18, for a closing that labels a video as internal rather than official: a plain card reading "Internal only", beside the official closings and None, for staff and students alike. It is the first card the app draws rather than decodes, so it builds the renderer the two title cards (VH-127, VH-128) reuse, in the closing slot the pipeline already has.

## Acceptance criteria

- A closing choice "Internal only" beside the official types and None (`ClosingType`); choosing it appends a silent card by a hard cut, and the onset control is unread for it, as for Cut.
- The card is drawn in the worker: text into frames at the output's size and frame rate, held for a duration set in `src/config/`, silent on the source's channel count; no new file under `public/branding/`, no new runtime dependency.
- The card is opaque, carries no University logo, and shows the date it was made, so it cannot be lifted as a generic stamp; nothing about it reads as the official closing.
- Its text meets 7:1 against its ground, in a face the app already ships or the system provides (Lora, Arial).
- The size and time estimates count the card; loudness is still measured on the source only; the boundary fade (D3) applies at the join.
- The page's new words are in all three language tables, by VH-105's sheet route.
- Spec §4 says so, in text the owner approves (docs/ is protected).
- `npm run check` green; checked in Chrome on a 1080p and a 4K sample.

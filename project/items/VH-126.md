# VH-126 — The Internal use only closing

## Intent

The owner asked on 2026-10-14 for a closing that clearly labels a video as internal, and on 2026-10-18 settled it: the official closing, still Nottingham Blue and branded, with the words "Internal use only" added, pre-built as new source media from the masters. It sits beside the official closings and None, for staff and students alike. Its words are the first text the band sets, so this item also settles the type scale and layouts all three cards use.

## Acceptance criteria

- First, before any asset: the type scale and layouts for this closing's words and for both title cards (VH-127, VH-128), measured in Chrome with worst-case text in all three languages and shown to the owner — highly readable, with plenty of room, and no text under 28 pt at PowerPoint sizes, 56 px per 1080 lines of the frame's shorter side (the owner, 2026-10-18).
- New closing assets with "Internal use only" added, built from the masters by `scripts/build-branding.mjs` beside the existing twelve, at both heights; the masters in `samples/` are read, never written.
- The words meet 7:1 against the ground, in a brand face (Lora or Arial), clear of the logo.
- On screen, choosing Internal use only gives the official closing's behaviour with the new assets; the estimates are unchanged, since the closing's length is.
- The page's new words are in all three language tables, by VH-105's sheet route.
- Spec §4.1, §4.2 and §12 say so, in text the owner approves (docs/ is protected).
- `npm run check` green; checked in Chrome on a 1080p and a 4K sample, in each mode the closing offers.

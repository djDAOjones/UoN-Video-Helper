# VH-154 — Page language moves into the header band on the right; the two video switchers stay under the lede

## Intent

The design pass (V-18) found three switchers above step 1, so "Choose file" sat below a 900 px window. Page language is about the page, not the video, so it moves into the header band, on the right, where the University's own site carries its utility links. Video type and Video language stay under the lede, because they shape the steps. The owner agreed on 2026-10-22 (VH-143.1).

## Acceptance criteria

- Page language sits in the header band, on the right of the logo, at desktop width; at phone width it wraps under the logo, inside the band, with no sideways scroll at 280 px.
- At desktop the band does not grow: it stays the logo plus its clear space.
- The heading is the first thing in `main`. The machine-translation note stays in `main`, above the heading, on a Chinese or Malay page.
- The switcher still works as before: native radios, each name in its own language, its live line, focus kept on the radio, never locked.
- It reads as light on Nottingham Blue, as the band's roles say. Under forced colours the band keeps its colours and the segments take the theme's, with a focus ring that shows on the blue.
- The skip link passes over the switcher to `main`.
- Tests pin the switcher's place and the band's layout; `npm run check` green, with no test deleted or skipped; checked in Chrome at 1280 px and 390 px.

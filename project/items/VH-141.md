# VH-141 — Nine small page defects: a locked box that looks live, a lifetime that reverts, two silent reveals, lost warnings, a dropped place, a stale block, stale progress, a wordless wait

## Intent

V-03, V-04, V-07, V-10, V-11, V-12, V-13, V-14 and V-15 of the 20 October audit, each rank 2, each a breach of a named rule that costs someone something today, each a small change in `src/main.ts`, `src/styles/app.css` or the dialog's markup. Together they finish what VH-124 and VH-135 set out: a page that is right about the moment after the one it was drawn for.

## Acceptance criteria

- V-03: `.textarea:disabled` takes the same treatment as `.select:disabled` and `.text-input:disabled` — no fill, a dashed edge, the AAA text pair — in the light, dark and forced-colours rules; the Student ID box reads as locked during a job and a save.
- V-04: a kept result that was delivered as a download keeps saying so however it is drawn again — after Keep it, after another file is chosen, after a withdrawn discard question; `lifetimeOf` starts from the result's delivered flag.
- V-07: the feedback dialog's "only in the copy" line is outside the English diagnostics block or in a span with the page's `lang`; the diagnostics themselves stay marked English.
- V-10: revealing the Errors captured panel, and a preview the player refuses, are each announced through a live region that is on the page in that state, with a short sentence that says what next; a "Set … here" button the preview's failure disables hands focus on when it held it.
- V-11: the kept result carries its output warnings (a closing that fell back, a track not carried) and shows them with its record, apart from the next file's pre-flight notes; choosing another file or re-checking does not remove them.
- V-12: when a save completes, focus is handed on before the scratch clean-up, from Stop saving or Save to the result, as the rule in UI-STANDARDS says; the clean-up is unchanged.
- V-13: a storage block, visible and spoken, is withdrawn when a re-check starts, and does not survive a stopped or failed re-check; Check again stays.
- V-14: once Cancel is pressed or a failure is shown, progress reports no longer move the bar, the stage line, the time left or the tab title; while the worker settles after a failure the page says that stopping is finishing, keeps the safety locks, and the keep-awake notice goes; the terminal reply is still handled. The 2.2.2 reading for the moving stage, bar and time-left lines is decided here and recorded as a spec wish line.
- V-15: while the load-time checks run, step 1's own status area says so in one line; it clears when they land. An unreadable file chosen after a readable one withdraws steps 2 to 6 or says why they stay, and restores the chooser's emphasis.
- Every new string is in all three language tables through the strings sheet; tests for each by category; `npm run check` green with no new skip; checked in Chrome at 1280 px and 375 px, with a screen-reader check of V-10 and V-12 as a wish line.
- V-21 and V-22 from the design pass (2026-10-21): the download's lifetime said once, and a failed save as an error notification, as the dated note of 2026-10-21 says.

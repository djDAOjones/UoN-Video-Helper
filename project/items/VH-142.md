# VH-142 — The words: the Chinese save messages and step 6, the damage note's tense, the errors panel's button

## Intent

V-08, V-16 and V-17 of the 20 October audit: three wording defects. On the Chinese page a stopped or cancelled save says the video can be watched any time where the English says it is still here to save, and step 6's heading and its button use two different making words. The decode-damage note's heading says a moment "has been silenced" above a body that says it "will be silent", and its times do not say they are the original recording's. The Errors captured panel tells the reader to use "the Send feedback button" while "Report this problem" sits under it.

## Acceptance criteria

- `status.saveStopped` and `status.notSaved` in `src/i18n/zh-Hans.ts` say the video is still here to save, as the English and Malay do; step 6's heading and "Create the video" use one making word in Chinese; both through the strings sheet, marked as the agent's drafts for the colleague.
- The decode-damage heading says the moment will be silenced, in all three languages, and the detail says the times are in the original recording.
- `failure.captured` names "Report this problem" in all three languages.
- Tests by category; `npm run check` green with no new skip; the readability gate green; checked in Chrome in all three languages.
- V-27 and V-28 (in part) from the design pass (2026-10-21): each table's marks made one, and one name for the job and the tool, as the dated note of 2026-10-21 says.

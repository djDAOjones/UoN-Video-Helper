# VH-153 — The words after VH-148: the trim intro's verb, the browser sentence, "(new tab)", the sheet's notes

## Intent

VH-148 replaced "closing" with "closing graphics" word for word, and the run's Chinese and Malay are the agent's drafts. The review of Codex's 21 October run found words that disagree:

- the English trim intro's verb;
- the browser sentence's translations, which reword the part all three sentences share;
- the footer's "(opens in a new tab)", against the page's own "(new tab)";
- the sheet's notes, which describe placeholders that have moved;
- Chinese and Malay step-6 lines that disagree with their button;
- uneven Chinese spacing;
- a Chinese stopped save that reads as a failure;
- dead and duplicate rows.

The item goes last in Phase 5 for two reasons. VH-150 removes strings and may add some, and this item then matches their notes once. And the writing review (PLAN-4.1) proposes rewording three of the same rows, which the owner may judge first.

## Acceptance criteria

R2-n is finding n under Rank 2 in the review. Rank-3 findings are named by their words there.

- **R2-7.** `trim.intro` says "…and the closing graphics are added…" in `src/i18n/en-GB.ts` and in `index.html`'s static English.
- **R2-8.** The three browser sentences share their common part word for word in each language:
  - Chinese: 此应用 … Chrome 浏览器;
  - Malay: "direka bentuk … pemeriksaan".
- **R2-9.** The footer's Creators team link says "(new tab)", the page's own phrase, in the three tables and in `index.html`. It is pinned beside the Equality Advisory link's in `src/ui/accessibility-statement.test.ts`.
- **Rank 3, the Creators team.**
  - `CREATORS_TEAM_URL`'s JSDoc in `src/config/feedback.ts` names the learning content team's own team.
  - The footer test checks the whole address the hand-off gave, not only the Teams host.
- **R2-10 and rank 3's repeated note.** The sheet's notes match their cells:
  - `source.readSummary` has no `{facts}` note;
  - `titleCard.submission.idsHelper`'s note has no `{max}`, and `idsHelperNext` has a note;
  - the four continuation rows say where they show;
  - `src/i18n/sheet.ts`'s translator notes say "the closing graphics";
  - the note that repeats itself says it once.
- **Rank 3, Chinese step 6.** The status lines use 制作 (正在制作视频, 视频无法制作). Malay's `couldNotCreate` uses the button's verb (dicipta), not "dihasilkan".
- **Rank 3, Chinese spacing.** A space on each side of a Latin word, and none before a full-width bracket: 此 Chrome 浏览器, 更新 Chrome 浏览器, MP4 视频（.mp4）.
- **Rank 3, Chinese stopped saves.** The stopped and cancelled saves offer saving again without reading as a failure. VH-142's pin in `src/i18n/tables.test.ts` takes the new words and still requires the offer.
- **Rank 3, `preset.details.smaller`.** The Chinese cannot be read as uploading the messages too.
- **Rank 3, Prettier and dead rows.**
  - `src/i18n/en-GB.ts` matches Prettier: single quotes, and `idsHelperNext` indented.
  - `source.variableFrameRate` is retired from the three tables and the sheet.
  - `saveFailedWhat` and `saveFailedNext` no longer repeat `saveFailed`, so each sentence has one row.
- **The gate.**
  - Every changed Chinese and Malay row is marked as the agent's draft in the sheet, through `scripts/strings-table.mjs`.
  - Tests by category.
  - `npm run check` green, with no test deleted or skipped.
  - Each changed row checked in Chrome in its language.

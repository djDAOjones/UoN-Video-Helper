# VH-130 — The footer's contacts and the accessibility statement

## Intent

The owner asked on 2026-10-18 for a footer that points people to the creators team and the learning content team by email, and links to an accessibility statement still to be written. The status they gave: "basic checks complete (10/10 on WebAIM WAVE), further tests to be determined". Staff who need help, or who meet a barrier, should find a person to write to and an honest account of how accessible the tool is, in the language the page is in.

## Acceptance criteria

- The footer shows the creators team and the learning content team as `mailto:` links, with names and addresses from `src/config/`, and a link to the accessibility statement.
- The statement is on the app's own page, reached from the footer, not a separate HTML page; it follows the structure of the GOV.UK model accessibility statement (Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) Accessibility Regulations 2018) in the page's plain English: who runs it and the aim; how accessible it is; known limitations (Chrome only; Chinese and Malay are machine translations checked on the live page; Chinese uses the computer's own font; the recorded exceptions to the AAA target); feedback and contact; the enforcement procedure; compliance status (partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 AA); how it was tested (the WAVE check and its date, and the project's own checks); planned further tests, to be decided by the owner; prepared, reviewed and next-review dates.
- Every visible string is in the language tables; the statement is translated in full into Chinese and Malay, drafted by the agent in the filled sheet and generated by `scripts/strings-table.mjs`.
- `npm run check` green with no new skip; checked in Chrome at 1280 px and 375 px in all three languages; the new English passes the readability gate.

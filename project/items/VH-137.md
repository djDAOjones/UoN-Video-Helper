- The footer's two team links use the creators team's and the learning content team's own addresses from `src/config/`, once the owner gives them; until then the owner's address stands.

  - the reading-level claim scoped to English;
  - the preview limitation gaining descriptions and alternatives;
  - UoN among the short forms;
  - the next tests split into those §13 requires and the optional tools;
  - "colleagues are checking" given as unconfirmed;
  - "partially compliant" tied to the open criteria.
- The owner's review settles the statement's two lists (design pass V-24; from VH-143.2). The recommendation is that the five abilities and six next tests take a capital where the language has one and the language's stop on all eleven, and the three straight apostrophes become curly ("computer's"). Following the GOV.UK model's structure is not itself an exception.
- The statement is true for the public release, 0.2.0 (PLAN-6): it names what was tested, and the work goes no further than what was checked.
- Every changed string is in all three language tables; `npm run check` green with no new skip; checked in Chrome in all three languages.

# VH-173 — The second pass judged: the journeys, visual design, UX and WCAG 2.2 AAA

## Intent

PLAN-7's third review stage. From VH-172's evidence, with Codex astra's blind review saved, Claude judges the page as one design and one journey by the plan's lenses and drafts its findings. It also writes the one-page checklist of what only a person can verify, for the Disability Support Services colleagues the owner is asking. It changes no product code.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Codex's blind review is saved first.** No finding enters the checkout before it is.
- **The journeys.** J1 to J4 each judged as a whole: action prominence, decision burden, reading order, repetition and continuity; page height per state as context against 20 October and the pass's starting build.
- **Visual design, Carbon productive.** Hierarchy, rhythm, alignment and density per scenario; every text element on one of the eight roles; every block of running text within the 28rem measure; one look per component kind in every state; dark and forced colours as designed states.
- **UX, Nielsen's ten.** One row per heuristic with a verdict and evidence; the conveyor's flow; prevention and recovery per failure and warning; the wording against UI-STANDARDS, one name per thing across the page and three languages.
- **WCAG 2.2 AAA.** The 86 criteria, each with applicability, scoped evidence, a verdict, any unresolved part and the 20 October verdict beside it; each gap typed as a defect, an accepted limitation or missing evidence; 1.4.8's five parts; raw mixed-script line counts with the reading stated.
- **Draft findings** from V-40 in VH-163's format (saw, standard, direction, source, evidence, rank), each naming its heuristic, criterion or Carbon rule; no duplicate of V-01 to V-39, and a regression reported under its old ID. A direction that would add a control, checkbox or element is marked for the owner and recommended against.
- **The person checklist**, one page in plain words, as the plan's "What a person must still check" sets it: the build link, test clips, tasks with expected outcomes, a report form and the feedback address from `src/config/feedback.ts`.
- No product code changes; the gate and the checker green.

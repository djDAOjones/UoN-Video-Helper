# VH-131 — Staff video or Student submission: the switch at the top

## Intent

The owner asked on 2026-10-19 for a switch at the top of the page between Student submission and Staff video. A student submission has the Submission card with its details and every logo animation option, always with Internal use only; a staff video has a basic card (title, department, author(s) and date, all optional), with Internal use only a toggle among its graphic options.

## Acceptance criteria

- A switch at the top of the page, Staff video or Student submission, in native radios, its words in all three language tables by VH-105's sheet route.
- Student submission: the Submission card (VH-128) is on and cannot be turned off; the closing is always Internal use only (VH-126), with Cut, Fade, Slide, the onset and the colour still offered.
- Staff video: the basic card (VH-127), the closing's options as now, and Internal use only a toggle beside them.
- Switching keeps every typed value, the trim, a running job and a result, as the language switcher does.
- The size and time estimates follow the switch.
- Spec §2, §4 and §9.1 say so, in text the owner approves (docs/ is protected).
- `npm run check` green; checked in Chrome on each side, in each of the three languages.

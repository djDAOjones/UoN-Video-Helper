# VH-184 — The onset toggletip closes on Tab, and its rail stops reading like a warning's

## Intent

Step 4's toggletip, the help beside the onset choice, stays open when focus moves on with Tab, so it can cover what comes next, and its coloured rail looks like a warning's, so help reads as a problem. This item closes it when focus leaves and gives the rail help's look rather than a warning's. It needs no new words and no new control, so it can start before the wording sheet is approved (PLAN-8). VH-189 later renames the label it sits beside; the behaviour here does not depend on the words.

## Acceptance criteria

- With the toggletip open, Tab or Shift+Tab out of it closes it, and focus goes where it would have gone; Escape still closes it and returns focus to its button; a pointer click outside still closes it.
- Its rail no longer uses the warning's colour or form; it uses an existing token for help or neutral information, with 3:1 against its background in light, dark and forced colours. No new token unless the owner's design tokens lack one, which stops and asks.
- Nothing about what a screen reader hears when it opens changes.
- Checked in Chrome at 1280, 375 and 320 px, in light, dark and forced colours, and in three languages; `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped; the digest rule applied named at close (rule 8); Codex astra checks the candidate diff before commit, and the commit carries its `Checked:` line.

# VH-129 — The student edition: no official closings, hosted apart

## Intent

A build for students that offers only None and Internal use only, so the student route cannot make a file that looks official. The owner deferred it on 2026-10-18 ("just one instance for now. we might separate later"); until then, staff and students share one app (PLAN-1).

## Acceptance criteria

- A build-time edition, beside the Xerte build's mode in `vite.config.ts`: the official closing code is left out of the student bundle and no closing master is copied into its package.
- The pipeline refuses an official closing in the student build, not only the page (`brandingChoiceFor`).
- A test holds the student package free of the masters and of the official choices.
- Hosting is settled with it (VH-14): while the staff app and its masters are public, a student edition is a nudge, not a control; the staff app behind sign-in, and the masters out of the public repository and the pilot, are the owner's call.

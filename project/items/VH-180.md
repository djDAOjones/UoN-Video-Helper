# VH-180 — The frame-rate note says only what is true

## Intent

One wording defect from the second design pass, in the string tables in three languages. The frame-rate note in Video properties always says frames "will be repeated" and names a rate. A fast source has frames dropped, and the Smaller preset uses another rate (V-47). On the owner's ruling it becomes one neutral sentence that is true both ways and names no rate, with no renderer change. V-45 (step 4's two names) is VH-181's, on the owner's ruling of 23 October. This item comes after VH-179 and before VH-181: it is strings and one call site, low risk, and its lines are measured on the CSS VH-179 leaves.

## Acceptance criteria

- **V-47.** `source.outputRate` becomes one sentence in each language that names no rate and is true whether frames are repeated or dropped. The English draft is "The output will run at a standard frame rate, so some frames will be repeated or left out." It is checked beside `variesNote`, which can show with it, so that the two do not say the same thing twice. With no placeholder the compiled message is a string, not a function (`src/i18n/compile.ts`), so the one call in `src/ui/source-panel.ts` (`:127`) reads `source.outputRate` instead of calling it. That is the only source change. The 10% threshold and the presets' behaviour stay as they are, no output shape is passed and no repaint is added.
- **V-47, checked both ways.** `src/ui/source-panel.test.ts` gains a source conformed up (`frameDeltaRatio` above 0.1) and one conformed down (below −0.1), each giving the neutral sentence. Live, the 45 fps and 120 fps fixtures, made in the scratchpad with ffmpeg as VH-174's were and never in `samples/`, are read under Larger and under Smaller in three languages. Before: VH-174's `d04` reading, "… so some frames will be repeated" at 120 fps.
- **V-18 not worsened.** `videoKind.result` sits above the chooser, so its new words can rewrap. The painted file button's bounds are read in J1's first screen at 1280 × 900 and 320 × 568, in three languages, against VH-179's after-reading. In none of the six cases does the button sit lower, and in English at 1280 it stays wholly inside the first screen. If any case is worse, the item stops and the owner is asked.
- **Measured, before and after, with the pass's walker:** every record that draws a changed string, at least S-03 and S-07 (the video type's line), S-15 (the trim intro and step 4), S-16 (Video properties), S-47 (the closings) and the finished result's record, with the measure, overflow, type and text-contrast instruments, in three languages and light, dark, forced-light and forced-dark; the leaf counts before and after. The table is kept in the bundle's measurements folder as vh180-words-2026-10-23.md. The language parity test (`src/i18n/tables.test.ts`) is green.
- **VH-176's stage rule, kept:** before and after measured with the pass's walker in three languages and light, dark and forced colours, with WCAG re-checked for what it touched (1.4.8, 1.4.10, and 3.1.5 by `test/readability.test.ts`); our own code on the two token files; no control, checkbox or element added; the progress bar kept.

# VH-182 — The footer's contact routes grouped by need: help, a problem, an opinion

## Intent

The foot of the page offers four routes, and two of them are called "feedback": the Send feedback button, which prepares an email with details that help find a problem, and the feedback form link, which asks what people think of the tool. Someone looking for help or reporting a fault cannot tell from the words which route is theirs. This item groups the routes by what the person wants (help using the tool, something not working, an opinion), renames the button for its job, and keeps each link's words standing alone. It waits until after the release, since the rename reaches the error messages and spec §9.2.

## Acceptance criteria

- The footer reads, in all three languages: a "Help and feedback" heading; under "Need help using the tool?", "Ask in the Creators team on Teams (new tab)" and "Email the learning content team"; under "Something not working?", the button "Report a problem"; under "What do you think of the tool?", "Fill in the feedback form (new tab)". The owner may change the words before work starts.
- The dialog's heading and every error message that names the button use its new name; no "Send feedback" is left in `src/`, `index.html` or the README.
- Each link still says alone where it goes and how it opens (WCAG 2.4.9); the group questions are headings or text, never part of a link's name.
- Spec §9.2, and §11 where it names the route, use the new name, by a correction the owner approves.
- The owner's two answers are recorded before work starts: whether students can open the Creators team link, and which address problem reports go to.
- Checked in Chrome at 1280, 375 and 320 px, light, dark and forced colours, in three languages; `npm run check` green with no new skip.

# VH-182 — The footer's contact routes grouped by need: help, a problem, an opinion

## Intent

The foot of the page offers four routes, and two of them are called "feedback": the Send feedback button, which prepares an email with details that help find a problem, and the feedback form link, which asks what people think of the tool. Someone looking for help or reporting a fault cannot tell from the words which route is theirs, and students cannot open the Creators team on Teams at all. This item groups the routes by what the person wants (help using the tool, something not working, an opinion), renames the button for its job, gives students their own help route through Self Service, and sends problem reports to the learning content team. The words are the VH-182 rows of the Phase 8 wording sheet; spec §9.2 and §11 change by corrections S22 and S24, which also carry VH-160.1.

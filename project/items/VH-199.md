# VH-199 — The third pass's evidence run: the matrix, and Phase 8's changes verified

## Intent

The third pass's second review stage, as VH-172 was the second's. With the instruments re-proved and Codex's method check applied, this item runs the matrix VH-197 sets, verifies every Phase 8 item against the Phase 8 contract and re-verifies Phase 7's fixes, keeping only the evidence a finding or verification needs. It writes no finding and changes no product code.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The matrix run** as the plan sets it: every walked scenario in three languages at 1280, 375 and 320 px in light; the finite list in dark, both forced palettes, the four enlargement records, 1.4.12 spacing, reduced motion, fallback faces and the landscape and short-height view; the keyboard route through the four journeys in three languages at 1280 px and in English at 320 px.
- **Accounted for.** Expected and observed coverage match, or each gap is named with its reason; no failed or missing record counts as a pass.
- **One digest per instrument**, with small supporting measurements; no digest repeats another; each beside Phase 7's after-run, every changed measurement explained.
- **The Phase 8 contract verified**: one row per Phase 8 item and per Phase 7 fix, with a verdict (holds, partial, regressed, unverified) and its measurement, in one table. A regression is reported under the item's own ID.
- **Captures** committed only where a verification cites them; the full run stays in the scratchpad.
- Claude's verdicts stay out of the checkout until Codex's blind review is saved.
- No product code changes; the gate and the checker green.

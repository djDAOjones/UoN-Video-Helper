# VH-209 — At a 32 px default font on a phone width, the page keeps to its column

## Intent

Pass 3 found that at a 32 px default font in a 320 or 401 px window the page scrolls sideways, in all six of S-113's records and all three languages, to 444–528 px (V-50, rank 3, contested 3 or 2). The records' offenders: the page language switcher's and Video language's "Bahasa Malaysia" at 320 px; Video type's student-submission choice at 320 px in English and Malay; Set start here and Set end here at 320 px in every language and at 401 px in Malay; the closing colour "White" at 401 px, and at 320 px in Chinese; and the Malay statement heading, "Kenyataan kebolehcapaian", at 401 px. No WCAG failure is established, since 1.4.10's condition is the default text size, but a person with a large font on a phone scrolls sideways to read the choices and reach Set. It was a proposal in VH-202's ask, and the owner took it at rank 3. CSS only, with the existing tokens; no element, no move of the switchers and no rewording.

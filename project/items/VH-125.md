# VH-125 — The trim preview follows the handle

## Intent

The owner asked on 2026-10-18 that the trim step show the point of the video in the preview while a handle moves. Until now the preview stayed where the player left it, so a member of staff set the times by number or played the video to find a cut; seeing the frame at the handle lets them choose the cut by eye.

## Acceptance criteria

- Dragging a handle, moving it by keyboard, or typing a valid start or end time shows that time in the preview, paused.
- A handle held at the other handle's limit shows the limit, not where the pointer is.
- During a drag the preview keeps drawing and comes to rest on the last time asked for; a time asked for one file is never shown on the next.
- "Set start here", "Set end here" and "Use the whole video" do not move the preview; no new words on the page, no announcement.
- `npm run check` green; checked in Chrome on a sample.

# VH-201 — The third pass's report: merged with Codex astra's blind review, and critiqued

## Intent

The third pass's report stage, as VH-174 was the second's. Claude's draft findings and Codex astra's blind review become one report, each of Codex's findings verified, and Codex argues with the merged draft before it is committed. Where they still disagree, both positions are kept for the owner. It changes no product code.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Merged.** Each D-nn finding verified live or in source and merged into a V-nn finding, kept as its own, or set aside with a reason; a table maps D-nn to V-nn.
- **The report**, a dated `design-pass-3` report in the bundle, in the design-pass-2 report's format: verdict, how it was done (with the manifest's coverage and what stayed unresolved), findings with ranks, the Phase 8 contract table, the journeys, the Nielsen and WCAG tables or links to them, "What held up", "Set aside, with reasons" and a Disposition table. The person checklist beside it as its own file.
- **Codex astra's critique** of the merged draft, from a brief, saved verbatim; each point applied, refused with a reason, or for the owner.
- **Disagreements.** One of fact settled by evidence with both views kept, or recorded "unresolved"; one of judgement listed with both positions under "For the owner", for VH-202's ask.
- No product code changes; the gate and the checker green; the commit carries `Checked: gpt-6-astra — <verdict> — the draft report`.

# VH-191 — The two look-alike language switches told apart

## Intent

Page language, in the header, and Video language, above step 1, offer the same three names in the same control, one above the other on a phone, so a person can change the wrong one. Video language already follows the page's language until changed. This item acts on the owner's choice in question Q4 of the wording sheet (VH-182.2): option 1, the recommendation, which moves Video language into step 3, where its words are drawn on the title card, beside the closing's words in step 4. It would appear only once a video is read, lock with step 3 while a video is made, and its helper would say where its words show. That also lifts the first action (VH-187). The words are row B9; spec §9.1 changes by correction S19, for the recommended option.

# VH-133 — The owner's translation corrections, merged

## Intent

On 2026-10-19 the owner returned the translation sheet with 21 cells reworded, 16 Chinese and 5 Malay. The live Chinese and Malay pages still show the wording they corrected. Staff reading those pages should see the corrected words, without losing the agent's 224 repairs or the 43 keys added since the sheet went out.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each of the returned sheet's 21 reworded cells is compared with the same key in `reviews/2026-10-18/VH-105-strings-filled.csv`, and lands there one by one, or is recorded in Notes with the reason.
- In the 14 cells the agent had already repaired, the wording that stands is the owner's choice, asked with both wordings side by side.
- `choose.dropHeld`'s "put down your phone" is put to the owner before it lands; `progress.title.ready` keeps `{app}`, and the placeholder test still refuses a renamed placeholder.
- `FILLED_SHEET` still names the filled sheet, never the returned one; every other cell, the 224 repairs and the 43 newer keys are unchanged.
- The tables are regenerated by `scripts/strings-table.mjs`; `npm run check` green with no new skip; each changed string checked in Chrome on its page.

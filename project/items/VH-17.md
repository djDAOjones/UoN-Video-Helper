# VH-17 — Evaluate `fastStart: 'reserve'` for the smaller preset

## Intent

the "smaller file" preset goes to OneDrive and SharePoint, where students may stream it. `fastStart: false` puts the moov box at the end, which can force a full download before playback starts.

## Acceptance criteria

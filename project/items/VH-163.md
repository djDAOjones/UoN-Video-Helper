# VH-163 — The design pass: one type scale, one reading measure, one grid, judged for design, UX and accessibility

## Intent

The owner sees more variety than the page needs in its type and in the widths its text sits at. VH-143 checked that every value comes from a token, and VH-145 closed the gaps it found. Its "What held up" called the type one ramp, and so it is. But the tokens are combined in more ways than the page has jobs. A 16 px heading comes in three weights and two line heights. Helper text stops 61 px short of the running text beside it. The column grows 151 px when the page turns to Chinese, and text starts at four different left edges in the main column. This pass judges the page as one design, step by step and state by state. It brings each kind of text to one named style and each kind of block to one width, and it re-checks WCAG 2.2 AAA and Nielsen's ten heuristics on everything it touches. The owner's rule holds throughout: the app stays simple. The pass consolidates and adds no control, checkbox or other element, and the progress bar stays (VH-150).

## Acceptance criteria

This item is the review, and it changes no product code. It plans its rank 1 and rank 2 fixes as items in Phase 6 ahead of VH-162, as VH-143 planned Phase 4 (PLAN-6, 2026-10-22).

- **A findings report.** A dated design-pass report in a new bundle under the reviews folder continues VH-143's numbering from V-31. It uses the audit's format: saw, standard, direction, source, evidence, and the same three ranks. It ends with "What held up", "Set aside, with reasons" and a Disposition table. Each finding names its heuristic, success criterion or Carbon rule.
- **The inventory, before.** It tallies the computed styles of every text element in every state walked: size, line height, weight, letter spacing, face and where each is used. It also lists every block's rendered width and left edge, and every margin, padding and gap that is not a `--spacing-*` step. The tally is kept in the bundle's evidence measurements, with the script that made it, so a fix item can take it again.
- **Judged: the type scale.** Each kind of text should take one named style: page title, step heading, section heading, label or legend, body, supporting text, status mark and code. Each style should be a Carbon productive token (`body-01`, `body-compact-01`, `heading-compact-01`, `heading-03`, `heading-04` and so on) with one size, weight and line height. Headings of one level should look the same wherever they appear: steps, statement, system check, errors, feedback and footer.
- **Judged: one reading measure.** All running text in a language should stop at one width, whatever its size, so helper text and notification bodies stop where the body does. The Chinese measure should keep WCAG 1.4.8's 40 glyphs without the column jumping when the language changes, or the report says why the jump stays. Controls, rows and panels should keep to one content column and one set of left edges per panel.
- **Judged: one grid.** The header band, the main column and the footer should share gutters at every width; at 375 px the band is inset 20 px and the main column 24 px. Whether the logo keeps the window's edge at 1280 px, as nottingham.ac.uk does (VH-154), goes to the owner as a question.
- **Judged: spacing.** Every margin, padding and gap should be a `--spacing-*` step or a named brand token, and the report says why each brand token sits outside Carbon's scale. Each step should have one rhythm: the same space between a heading and its text, a field and its helper, and one panel and the next.
- **Judged: hierarchy and grouping per step.** In each of the six steps, the switchers and the footer, the step's one action should lead, related controls sit together and the supporting text stay subordinate. The verdict, warnings and result stay one component each, as VH-124 set.
- **Judged: controls and states.** Buttons, segments, checkboxes, fields, selects and links should look and behave one way per kind. Every state is walked, with each state's one primary kept: rest, hover, focus, active, disabled, invalid, locked, running and finished.
- **Judged: Nielsen's ten heuristics**, end to end, from first load to a saved file and a second video, with a finding for each breach.
- **Judged: WCAG 2.2 AAA.** Text contrast at 7:1 (1.4.6). 1.4.8's visual presentation: the measure, unjustified text, space-and-a-half lines and the paragraph gap. 1.4.12 text spacing with nothing clipped. Reflow at 320 px (1.4.10) and 200% zoom (1.4.4) with no sideways scroll. Focus appearance (2.4.13) and focus never obscured (2.4.12). 44 px targets (2.5.5).
- **Judged in three languages and three colour modes.** English, Chinese and Malay: the Chinese measure, its full-width punctuation and the CJK face, and Malay's longer strings in the switchers, buttons and trim rows at 320 px. Light, dark and forced colours, with the band's focus ring the `--focus` white (VH-154).
- **Screenshots, before.** Captured at 1280 × 900 and at 375 × 812 with mobile emulation, in each of the three languages. They cover at least the empty page, a video read, the job running and the finished result, and are named by finding.
- **Codex's blind review and critique.** Codex writes a blind design review from the same brief, then critiques the merged draft. Both are kept as written, each of its findings is verified live or in source, and what holds is applied before commit (the owner's choice, PLAN-6, 2026-10-22).
- **The fixes planned.** Every rank 1 and rank 2 finding is planned with `verbs/plan.md` as items in Phase 6 ahead of VH-162, each with a file, in a PLAN-6 entry with an Items line. The owner approves the fix plan before any fix is built. Rank 3 findings go to the owner as proposals; any not taken now become wish lines.
- **What each fix item carries.** The inventory taken again after the fix, beside the before. The screenshots taken again, side by side with the before. The fix checked in all three languages and colour modes, with WCAG re-checked for what it touched. The fix stays in our own CSS on the two token files, adds no control or element, and keeps the progress bar. `UI-STANDARDS.md` names the type scale, the measure and the grid if any of them changes, within its 2,400-word budget or with the owner asked to raise it. A change to `docs/` is drafted as a wish line for the owner. The gate is green.
- **Close.** `node tools/check.mjs` and `npm run check` are green with no test deleted or skipped, and the close names the digest rules it applied (rule 8).

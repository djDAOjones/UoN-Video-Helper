# VH-157 — A title card with nothing typed is not made silently

## Intent

Ticking "Add a title card" fills the date, so pressing Create with nothing
typed makes a 5-second card that shows only the date, without a word
(VH-138.1; evidence `reviews/2026-10-20/evidence/aside-date-only-card-record.png`).
The owner asked for a warning and the accessible course of action, with
focus moved to the card's field. A staff card now needs a title,
department or authors. The date alone stops Create the way an empty card
already does, and the page gains no new control.

## Acceptance criteria

- `isCardEmpty` ignores the date: a staff card with only the date, typed or filled, stops Create as an empty card does
- the rule line under the box says first that a title, department or authors is needed, and that the date is filled in
- at Create, the line beside the box says to type a title, department or authors, or untick Add a title card; the status line says "Correct the title card in step 3 to continue."; focus moves to Title
- while that line shows, Title is `aria-invalid` and names the line in its `aria-describedby`, so a screen reader hears the reason on focus; typing in any of the three clears the line and the status
- Student submission is unchanged: its four required fields already refuse each missing one beside it, with the same status line and focus to the first
- Chinese and Malay rows changed are marked as the agent's drafts in the sheet
- the gate is green; checked in Chrome for both video kinds, one screenshot per state

# VH-143 — The design pass: formatting, UI and visual design against the 2026-10-01 standard

## Intent

The 20 October audit (VH-138) measured the live page for accessibility and usability and planned Phase 3 from what it found. It did not judge the page as a design: whether every length, colour and weight still comes from the two token files on Carbon's scale, whether the type and spacing read as one hierarchy now that three switchers, a notice, two title cards, an Internal use only closing, a decode-damage note, a statement and a time left have joined the page, whether every string in all three tables is formatted the same way, and whether the frames the app draws keep their type scale at every aspect. This pass does that, against the standard VH-124 set on 1 October, and plans what it finds as Phase 4. The owner asked for it on 2026-10-21 as the first step of an overnight, gateless run (decisions, 2026-10-21, VH-143); it changes no product code.

## Acceptance criteria

- The bundle gains `design-pass-2026-10-21.md`: findings V-18 onward in the audit's format (saw, standard, direction, source, evidence, the same three ranks), "What held up", and "Set aside, with reasons"; the audit's Disposition table and the bundle's README extended so every finding reads as one list.
- A token sweep of the computed styles of every element in every state walked, tracing each margin, padding, gap, font size, line height, border, radius and colour to a token in `src/styles/tokens.carbon.css` or `src/styles/tokens.brand.css` on Carbon's scale, with anything else a finding; kept in `reviews/2026-10-20/evidence/measurements/`.
- Hierarchy and rhythm judged: the type scale and weights in each state, paragraph and section spacing, alignment to one grid, the visual weight of the three switchers and the notice above step 1, and the page's growth since 1 October judged for distraction, not as a case for folding.
- One component for one job: the verdict, warnings, losses, failures and every note added since 1 October compared against VH-124's single inline notification; the same for buttons, segments and checkboxes.
- Every string in all three tables checked for capitals, full stops, punctuation and quotation marks, en-GB spelling, one word for one thing, numbers and units through `Intl`, Chinese full-width punctuation and spacing per the VH-122 decision, and Malay consistency; the verdict on meaning left to the native-speaking colleagues.
- Word counts per state against VH-124's after-states, kept in `reviews/2026-10-20/evidence/measurements/`; the Chinese 40-glyph measure checked on a state with long Chinese text, the statement.
- The title cards and closings at 16:9, 9:16, 3:4 and 4:3 in all three languages checked against their type scale (VH-127, VH-128).
- The same states as VH-124's after-captures captured again, side by side, with what has drifted since 1 October noted; screenshots in `reviews/2026-10-20/evidence/`, named by finding; ffmpeg fixtures only.
- Walked in headless Chrome with the bundle's walker rebuilt: 1280 × 900, 390 × 844 with mobile emulation, 320 px, text spacing, light, dark and forced colours; all three languages, both switch positions, a card on and off, every closing.
- Codex astra's blind design review from the same brief and its critique of the merged draft kept as written; each of its findings verified live or in source; what holds applied before commit.
- Every finding planned with `verbs/plan.md`: a finding on a Phase 3 item's lines joins that item's criteria through a dated note and a decision; the rest become `### Phase 4 — <name>` with items from the next free ID, each with a file; PLAN-4 recorded with its Items line and signed off gateless on the owner's 2026-10-21 instruction.
- `node tools/check.mjs` and `npm run check` green; closed per `verbs/close.md` with a `Checked:` line.

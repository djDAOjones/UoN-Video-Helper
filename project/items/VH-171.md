- **Scenarios.** The 64 states of 20 October become scenarios S-01 onward, each with setup, expected transition, resulting focus and status, and values kept, with the catalogue's "Meant to do", "Competes" and "Repeats" columns; every row mapped; each walked or source-only with the reason; the plan's additions included; the finite list for the other modes fixed.
- **The walker copied and extended.** From HEAD into the bundle's walker folder under its evidence measurements, its source revision named; the old bundle untouched. It gains what the plan's "The instruments" section lists: declared scenarios with asserted state, language and mode; fresh run directories that fail on missing, duplicate, stale or failed records; a "cannot determine" outcome; Chrome's accessibility tree; a keys-only keyboard route kept apart from setup; rendered focus area and change of contrast; actual target areas; contrast beyond text nodes; the four enlargement records; reduced motion; fallback faces.
- **Proved to detect.** Temporary failing fixtures in the scratchpad, never in `src/`: an overflow, a focus ring too small and a control with no accessible name, each caught by its instrument; the result recorded.
- **The manifest** in the bundle: source commit, instrument revision, browser and OS, viewport, scaling method, fixture identity, expected and observed coverage, unresolved checks.
- **A pilot**: a few scenarios through every instrument, in one language and three modes.
- **Codex astra's method check** of the instrument diff, the scenario list, the manifest and the pilot, saved verbatim with its brief; each point applied or refused before VH-172 starts.
- No product code changes; the gate and the checker green; the commit carries `Checked: gpt-6-astra — <verdict> — the instruments and pilot`.

# VH-132 — The video's text in any of the three languages

## Intent

The owner asked on 2026-10-19 that the words the app draws into the video, the cards' labels and the Internal use only closing, follow the page's language by default, with a selector on every language version to choose any of the three. What a person types on a card is drawn as typed, never translated.

## Acceptance criteria

- A selector for the video's text language on every language version of the page, set to the page's language until someone chooses another.
- The cards' labels and date (VH-127, VH-128) and the closing's words (VH-126) are drawn in the chosen language.
- Each option names its language in that language, with its own `lang`, as the page's switcher does.
- The page's new words are in all three language tables, by VH-105's sheet route.
- `npm run check` green; checked in Chrome with each page language against each video language.

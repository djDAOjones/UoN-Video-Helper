# VH-M4 — Watch staff use it

## Intent

what VH-104 could not judge, listed in the review's "What only a person can judge": a pilot session — three to five staff, their own recordings, unaided, Choose to Save, thinking aloud — plus screen readers, real phones, speech input, Windows high contrast and a managed University laptop. The pilot session answers VH-97, and whether "Cut" / "None" and "Larger / better" / "Smaller / reduced" mean to staff what they mean.

## Acceptance criteria

- at least three staff have been watched end to end, and what they stumbled on is filed as backlog items.
- The owner's part is named on the backlog line's blocker; nothing closes without it.

# VH-206 — A kept part with too little audio is told so, apart from a video with none, in the owner's words

## Intent

Pass 3's one rank 1 finding (V-51): VH-185 made a trimmed part whose decoded sound lasts under one 400 ms gating block a silent keep (`keptTooLittleSound`, `src/media/audio-plan.ts`, `:115–121`), and the page then says "This video has no audio" and "If you expected audio, check the recording" (`src/i18n/en-GB.ts`, `:670–674`). That is untrue when the part kept holds a little real sound: a person who hears it in the preview is told the recording has none and sent to the wrong place. The worker reduces every silent result to no analysis (`src/workers/job.worker.ts`, `:535`), and the warning builder gives both cases one `no-audio` code (`src/audio/warnings.ts`, `:92`), so one string cannot tell them apart. This item carries the omission's reason through that path into the existing warning, and shows the owner's approved words for the kept-part case; audio that is really absent keeps today's words. No control is added and the 400 ms threshold stays (`BLOCK_SECONDS`), as the owner's answer to VH-202's ask kept it (point 3).

## Acceptance criteria

- **The reason carried.** A trimmed keep that `keptTooLittleSound` makes silent is told apart from a source with no audio track, from `analyseSourceAudio`'s silent result (`'silent' in measured`, `job.worker.ts:534–535`) to `detectSourceWarnings` (`warnings.ts:92`) and `warningText` (`src/ui/warning-text.ts`, `:69`): a typed reason on the existing `no-audio` warning, or a second code, never a number in `detail`, which holds measured values (`warnings.ts:33–34`); the build chooses and says why, with the precedence of `cut-short` and `ends-early` unchanged. A trimmed keep with no decoded sound at all goes with the kept-part case, since the file has a track and keeping more is the recovery, unless the sound it declares never arrives, which stays `ends-early` (`warnings.ts:90–92`).
- **The owner's words.** The kept-part case shows the words the owner approved at VH-202's close (point 2, "as reccomended"), verbatim, the Chinese and Malay marked machine-translated as the ask marked them:
  - en-GB: heading "The part you kept has too little audio to level"; detail "The new video will have no audio. If you want the audio, move the start or end time to keep more of the video where there is audio."

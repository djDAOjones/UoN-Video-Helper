# VH-208 — The finished video's warnings come before its player and Save, and its listening advice is in the completion line

## Intent

Pass 3 found that the heavy-limiting warning's advice, "…so play the new video before you save it" (`src/i18n/en-GB.ts`, `:722`), comes after the action it informs (V-52, rank 2; Nielsen 5 and 6). The finished card holds its lines, the player and the lifetime line (`src/main.ts`, `:3285`), then Save (`:3494–3497`); the warnings follow the whole card (`processResult.replaceChildren(heading, resultWarnings)`, `:3327`). The completion's live-region write gives each warning's heading only (`:3073–3078`; `warningsSpoken`, `src/ui/announce.ts`, `:40–46`). A save then removes the player (VH-192), so the advice can no longer be taken. This item puts the existing warnings before the player and Save in the card's reading order, in one notification treatment, and has the completion line carry the existing listening sentence. It adds no element and no words, and VH-193's decision stands.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Before the player and Save.** In the finished card's reading order, DOM and visual alike, the output's warnings (`resultWarnings`, `main.ts:3287`) come after the card's title and lines and before the player, the lifetime line and Save. One notification treatment: never one notification nested in another, never two rails (VH-150). The same order holds for a "Previous video", after "Keep it", after a save and after a page-language change (`paint`, `:3300–3328`).
- **Spoken with the completion.** The completion's live-region write carries the heavy-limiting warning's existing listening sentence, from the existing strings: its existing detail spoken whole after its heading, so no string is new or split. With Announce progress off, the same line is written (S-151). Other warnings keep heading-only speech.
- **The pins moved, not dropped.** VH-150's regression test (`test/screen-text.test.ts`, `:310–314`, which asserts `processResult.replaceChildren(heading, resultWarnings)`) is rewritten to the new order and still forbids one notification nested in another; it is not deleted or skipped. The walker's `warnBox` (`reviews/2026-10-25/design-pass-3/evidence/measurements/walker/phase8.mjs`, `:59`), which finds the warnings as the card's next siblings, is qualified against the new layout before the after is read. The completion check, which tests warning titles (`phase8.mjs`, `:833–847`), and its qualification fixture, which passes heading-only text (`reviews/2026-10-25/design-pass-3/evidence/measurements/walker/prove-8.mjs`, `:159`), are made to reject heading-only speech for heavy-limiting and accept its full existing detail, with `src/ui/announce.test.ts` (`:31–35`) to match. The live region's write is exposure, not speech; what a screen reader says stays the person checklist's task A.

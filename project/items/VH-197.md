# VH-197 — The third design pass planned, with Codex astra's critique

## Intent

The owner asked for Phase 8 to end with a design, UX and accessibility analysis as thorough as the one that opened this work, Phase 7's second pass. Phase 8 has changed the page in fourteen places, among them a native video player, a limiting warning, a regrouped footer, a second toggletip and Video language in step 3. A single walk at the close, as VH-195 first planned, cannot show they hold together. This item designs the third pass from Phase 7's plan, has Codex astra critique the design, and puts it to the owner. It changes no product code.

## Acceptance criteria

- The pass designed in a dated `design-pass-3` bundle under `reviews/`, from `plan-2026-10-22.md`, and at least as broad in every dimension: the journeys J1 to J4 and their scenarios, three languages, the views, modes and keyboard; the instruments and how they are qualified; the four lenses; the Codex touchpoints with the rule for disagreements; the person checks for Disability Support Services; the stages VH-198 to VH-203.
- The scenarios carried over, and new ones for every state Phase 8 added or changed (VH-182 to VH-194 and VH-196), each traced to its item.
- A Phase 8 contract in place of the six-fix contract: for each Phase 8 item, what it changed, what must hold, and what neighbouring states it could have disturbed; Phase 7's fixes (VH-178 to VH-181) re-verified beside it.
- The matrix is no smaller than VH-172's run: every scenario once in its base cell, each variant where a check depends on it, and every cell a Phase 8 item touched in full. Any further reduction is the owner's call, asked with its reason.
- Codex astra's critique of the draft saved verbatim with its brief; each point applied or refused with a reason.
- The owner approves the plan before VH-198 starts.
- No product code changes; `node tools/check.mjs` and `npm run check` green.

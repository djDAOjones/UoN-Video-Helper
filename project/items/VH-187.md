# VH-187 — The top of the page: the promises say what happens, and the first action sits higher

## Intent

Above step 1 the page says "This tool does the following to your video:", then three vague promises ("Ensures consistent audio levels"), the privacy line, and a browser sentence that shows even when every check has passed, though the footer's System check already says "all passed". At 800 × 600 and 375 px, Choose a video sits a screen down. This item cuts the lead-in, makes the promises say what happens (INTAKE.4, VH-182.5), and shows the browser sentence only when a check has not passed (VH-182.1), so the first action rises. The words are rows B4 and B5 of the wording sheet; spec §10 changes by correction S17 and S18, which the owner approves on the sheet.

## Acceptance criteria

- The lede has no lead-in sentence; the three promises and `app.description` read as the sheet's B4 approves.
- The browser sentence shows only when the load-time check has not passed, in its present "not passed" words; while the check runs, step 1's own "Checking whether this browser can run the tool…" is the only status, and when it passes nothing stands above step 1 but the lede, the privacy line and the switches (sheet B5). `browserNote.pending` and `browserNote.passed` are removed from the three tables and the sheet.
- A browser that fails or warns on any load-time check is still told, above step 1: `browserNote.notPassed` shows in its present words in three languages, whether the check settles before or after a video is chosen. A fail or warn already observed takes precedence over checks still pending, so `browserNote` tests for them first, and `src/ui/system-check.test.ts` keeps a fail case, a warn case, and mixed cases with `pending` beside `fail` and beside `warn`, all returning it, beside the all-pending and all-passing cases that now show nothing. This is the owner's condition on Q8.
- No layout jump on a passing load: the measured position of the file button at 1280 × 900, 800 × 600, 375 and 320 px, before and after, in three languages, is recorded, and it is never lower after.
- Spec §10 reads as corrections S17 and S18 approve, applied in this item's commit, and the spec stays within its 8,100 words.
- The English is the sheet's, as the owner approved it; the Chinese and Malay rows are the agent's machine drafts, through the filled sheet and `scripts/strings-table.mjs`, marked so for a native reader; `src/i18n/tables.test.ts` is green, and any key removed goes from all three tables and `src/i18n/sheet.ts` in the same commit.
- Checked in Chrome at 1280, 375 and 320 px, in light, dark and forced colours, and in three languages; `npm run check` green with no test deleted or skipped; the digest rule applied named at close (rule 8); Codex astra checks the candidate diff before commit, and the commit carries its `Checked:` line.

## After Codex astra's critique

Codex astra critiqued this plan before the owner's ask
([`codex-astra-fixplan-critique-2026-10-27.md`](codex-astra-fixplan-critique-2026-10-27.md),
kept as written, from
[`codex-astra-fixplan-critique-brief-2026-10-27.md`](codex-astra-fixplan-critique-brief-2026-10-27.md)).
Its verdict: "Approve after the musts below". Five points, all musts, all
applied; none refused.

- **P-01, VH-207's capture comes too late for two of its promises.
  Applied.** Choosing the file already runs `clearTrim('reading')`, whose
  lock disables the trim, and re-renders a standing result, replacing its
  Save (`src/main.ts:1432`, `:1443–1444`, `:2462–2469`). VH-207 now
  captures at both moments, the choosing and the failed read: a trim
  control goes to the file input, a standing Save to its re-rendered
  self. Its variants cover both timings, the dialog and a reader. The
  exception path gets a named inspection-failure fault, since F14 and F11
  cannot reach it.
- **P-02, the before is not all in VH-199's records. Applied.** Each item
  keeps VH-199's records only where the condition matches, and measures
  every other before cell on its own base commit first. The 32 px font
  in dark and the forced modes is a combined cell the walker does not
  make yet, since a colour mode resets the font (`walker/qualify.mjs:995–1007`).
  VH-209 and VH-210 declare and qualify it first, a verification change
  only. VH-209 reads V-18's six cases on its base before the CSS, not
  against VH-203's later reading.
- **P-03, the walker's warning checks encode the old behaviour.
  Applied.** VH-206 moves S-99's state and wait to the new words, and has
  S-101's and S-153's negative checks reject them too, with S-20 unchanged.
  It adds the zero-decoded keep, and the declared sound that never
  arrives, to its tests. VH-208's completion check and its qualification
  fixture now reject heading-only speech for heavy-limiting, with
  `src/ui/announce.test.ts` to match, exposure kept apart from speech.
- **P-04, the written dependencies stopped VH-207 leading. Applied.** The
  plan's approval is what the uncontested items wait for. A partial answer
  may leave V-51's words open, and VH-202 then closes with VH-206 blocked
  on its own line. F15b's words, if he rules it a finding, are drafted
  for him and wait on a line of their own; VH-208 is not held for them.
- **P-05, Codex's V-18 position was stated too broadly. Applied.** The ask
  now gives both positions: Claude's (the acceptance stands now) and
  Codex's (provisional until today's position is measured). It says the
  geometry is unmeasured since VH-205 and that 320 × 568 means all three
  languages.

From "What held up", one wording was also taken: VH-206 keeps an

## 2. The walker change, declared and proved

The candidate changes only S-140 and S-140n in [`walker/scenarios.mjs`](walker/scenarios.mjs): one block (`:1533–1684`) and their two entries (`:2521`, `:2532`). Codex astra's checks found faults four times, and VH-214 #3, #5, #7 and #9 repaired them (see "The repairs" below). This section describes the candidate as it now stands.

- **`QT_BOUND`** (`:1580`): one deadline per phase, taken as the phase starts. The read gets 330 s from the choosing: the page's own 120 s for inspection and 180 s for the pre-flight (`src/config/thresholds.ts`, `:163–164`), plus 30 s. The job gets 900 s from Create. The boot, before the choosing, has its own 60 s, longer than `walk.mjs`'s 30 s, and does not count as the bound. A snapshot is read within 10 s.
- **`QT_OUTCOME`** (`:1581`): the accepted outcomes:
  - *refused before Create*: step 1's block, of kind error, with Create not shown;
  - *finished*;
  - *failed with recovery*: a failure shown, the job stopped, the file chooser enabled again.
- **`byDeadline`** (`:1589`): every wait and every read of the page in a phase is raced against that phase's deadline, so a page that never answers stops there too. The deadline is absolute, always: a result accepted at or after it counts as its expiry, which carries that late value. The deadline's timer rejects one turn of the event loop after it fires (`setImmediate`; both are cleared when the race ends), so an answer already waiting when the walk's own process was held is taken, and told late, not lost as no answer. This deferral is VH-214 #9's addition, in the block, beyond the third follow-up's words. The snapshots, `qtSnap` (`:1606`) and `qtEvidence`'s read, are strict the same way.
- **`qtLate`** (`:1599`): a snapshot's expiry that carried a late answer is dated from the snapshot's deadline, by the page's own time of the answer (`pageMs`) and by the walk's taking of it (`deliveredMs`). Dated before that deadline, it is late delivery (`lateDelivery`): the page answered in time, and the walk took the answer late. Dated at or after it, it is a late answer (`lateAnswer`), the page's. `qtSnap` reads the page's time with its answer, and it and `qtEvidence` keep either on a strict rejection.
- **`QT_RECORDER`** (`:1584`): installed at the choosing, inside the read's deadline. It logs each change of the outcome as observed, on a DOM change or a 50 ms tick, with the page's time it was first seen and the previous observation's time, so the change lies between the two. It keeps at most 200 entries; after that it keeps the first dropped entry and a count of the rest.
- **`quickTime`** (`:1631`) is the setup of both scenarios. It records each phase's deadline.
  - **Normal capture:** `qtCapture` (`:1611`) reads the outcome inside the phase's deadline. If the page dates its reading at or after the deadline, that is the expiry.
  - **At the read's or the job's deadline:** it keeps `expired`, with what ended the phase. An error at or after the deadline in those phases counts as the expiry too: for example, an inner wait's own "timeout waiting for", whose timer can fire first when the event loop runs late.
  - **The state at the deadline:** `atExpiry` comes from `qtEvidence` (`:1617`). The log is brought up to date and read within 10 s, strictly: an answer after that is no answer. The state at the deadline is the last entry first seen before it. An entry whose span crosses the deadline is kept as `ambiguous`; an overflow whose first dropped change may lie before the deadline is kept as unusable. An entry whose previous observation is at or after the deadline is kept as `late`, never as evidence of the state at the deadline. A page that does not answer is kept as not answering. An answer it dated inside the snapshot's bound, but the walk took after it, is kept as late delivery.
  - **Everything else:** the boot's end, or any other error before an outcome, is kept as `walkError`.
- **`qtJudged`** (`:1668`) is each scenario's first assert row; the old row is kept second. It judges what was kept, not what the page shows later (`later`, kept for the record, which reads "delivered late" when that snapshot's answer was delivered late too):
  - `walkError` reads cannot-determine, and names the walk;
  - a deadline reached with the snapshot's answer delivered late reads cannot-determine, and names the walk: the answer was dated in time but taken late. This is judged first, before the page's not answering;
  - a deadline reached with the log's overflow unusable, or with an entry ambiguous at it, reads cannot-determine, and names the walk, never the page;
  - a deadline reached, with an accepted outcome in the page's log before it, reads cannot-determine, and names the walk;
  - a deadline reached with none before it fails and names the page, even if one comes after;
  - a deadline reached with the page not answering, or answering only at or after the snapshot's bound by its own time, fails and names the page;
  - otherwise an accepted outcome passes, and none fails, naming no hang.
- **The proof's hook**, `globalThis.__QT_BOUND_MS`, shortens the deadlines for the proof only.

**Not bounded:** the runner's reads after the setup, the probe's `readModes` and the walker's state check. The bound ends the setup, but these later reads could still stop a verdict from being recorded; the recorded cells are unaffected. Bounding them is outside the owner's approval.

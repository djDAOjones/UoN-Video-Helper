## 5. The build, and what the variants do not walk

- **At the choosing,** one capture sits at the top of the change handler (`src/main.ts`), before anything is withdrawn. Focus on any of these goes to the file input:
  - the trim's controls the reading lock disables: the six, and "Use the whole video" (`renderTrim`);
  - the check's Stop and Check again, which `offerStop(null)` hides;
  - the read summary and its disclosure, which `clear(sourceReport)` empties;
  - the block panel, the verdict and its warnings;
  - Create's row (`processActions`).

  Focus in the result goes to the same control in the result shown again: Save to the re-rendered Save, the player to the same player, and the card to the re-rendered card. Focus on the "discard it?" question goes to the card it restores, the successor "Keep it" already has. A saved card that `clearResult` takes away goes to the file input.
- **On a read failure,** one capture sits in `clearTrim('unreadable')`, at its top, before `applyControlLock()`: `focusHeldBy(stepsWithdrawnBy({ setup: false, create: resultStanding }))`. Once `revealLaterSteps` has run, focus goes to the file input, or to Stop saving when a save under way has shut the chooser. It sits once because both paths, the failed reply and the exception, call `clearTrim('unreadable')`, and they are its only callers with that notice.
- **No other caller changes.** All 15 other `focusHeldBy` sites were read, and none was edited. `renderResult`, `clearResult`, "Keep it" and `withdrawDiscardQuestion` are unchanged.
- **The unit test.** The capture lives in `main.ts`, which `focus-hand-on.test.ts` cannot reach. So the walker's variants are its proof, and (c) walks the exception path. `focus-hand-on.test.ts` passes, 13 of 13.
- **Proved by reading only.** The player, the card, the question, the cleared card and the handler's earlier withdrawals are not walked by any variant. The approved walker changes are fixed at (a) to (f), and these are reachable only with focus off the chooser at the choosing.

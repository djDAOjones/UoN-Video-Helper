Pre-flight read in light, dark, forced-light and forced-dark, at the moment before Create (the probe intercepts the click on Create or Continue anyway, so S-100's, S-101's and S-153's setups run unchanged). A job cell then runs the job in light, reads the result in each of the four modes, clicks Save (the walker's OPFS save picker), copies the saved file out and reads its tracks with `ffprobe`.

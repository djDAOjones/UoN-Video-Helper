**Part B — findings and direction**

1. **No-audio notice: stands, rank 1.** S-99 reaches the notice ×31 and S-100 finishes ×6; output tracks were not inspected (`M/contract-2026-10-27.md:18`). Independently, the source classifies genuine decoded sound under 400 ms as silent (`src/media/audio-plan.ts:104`, `:120`, `:350`), while claiming absence (`src/i18n/en-GB.ts:670`). This can mislead a novice and damage trust. **Nielsen 1, 2 and 9.** Reuse the warning to distinguish omitted short sound, explain the consequence and suggest keeping more. No new element or threshold change.

2. **Limiting-warning placement: stands, rank 2.** S-135–S-138/S-151 establish warning presence, persistence and title announcements, not useful placement. The player and Save precede the warning container (`src/main.ts:3285`, `:3327`); speech contains headings only (`src/ui/announce.ts:42`). **Nielsen 5 and 6; UI-STANDARDS contextual guidance.** Move the existing warning before player and Save; include its existing listening instruction in completion speech. Preserve Save availability and unchanged-after-save wording. No new element.

3. **S-149 / V-46: stands, rank 2.** Three measured failures establish lost task position, not lost work. **WCAG 2.4.3; UI-STANDARDS:245.** Capture displaced focus before either unreadable-file withdrawal and use the existing hand-off to the chooser, preserving focus elsewhere and in the dialog.

4. **Onset “?”: stands, rank 2.** S-113 ms-MY w320 font32 records **460/464 px²** (`M/digest-focus-2026-10-27.md:35`). **WCAG 2.4.13.** Neither rounding nor a presumed measurement tolerance closes it. Adjust existing ring geometry or clearance; the precise lost-pixel mechanism remains unproved. No new element.

5. **Creators link: stands, rank 2.** Eight records show **1,108/1,264** or **1,595/1,968 px²**. This is a rectangular `inline-flex` component, not a fragmented inline link; its lower outline crosses the following link’s text (`src/styles/app.css:210`; `M/matrix-2026-10-27.md:95`). **WCAG 2.4.13.** Adjust existing spacing/outline placement while retaining the target size.

6. **V-18: narrowed, still rank 2.** The [painted-button readings](</Users/joe/Library/CloudStorage/OneDrive-TheUniversityofNottingham/_Joe Bell UoN Files/2_Projects/2026-08-24 UoN Video Helper/reviews/2026-10-25/design-pass-3/evidence/measurements/readings-2026-10-27/rings-179-v18.json:12>) agree with the contract’s VH-179 row: desktop **650–694, 800–844, 890–934**; narrow **950–994, 1,045–1,089, 1,287–1,331**. All narrow cases remain below the first screen. **Nielsen 8; Carbon productive/task-focused layout.** Further compression or reordering of existing introductory content is an **owner layout decision**.

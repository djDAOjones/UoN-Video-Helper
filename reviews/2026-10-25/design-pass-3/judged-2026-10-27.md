## Verdict

The Phase 8 page holds together as one design and one journey. Each state keeps one filled action, and it is the right one. Every text element is on a type role (19,138 rows, none failing), and every Phase 8 string matches the approved sheet. The run read 3,207 records in three languages, seven views and eight modes. Of the contract's eighteen rows, 12 hold, 5 are partial with their gap named, and one regressed (VH-191).

Phase 8 made the page taller where it added something to read or use:

- the steps are about 158 px taller, for Video language in step 3;
- the finished card is about 472 px taller, for the player;
- the statement is about 288 px taller, for VH-137's update and VH-182's contact line, not separable;
- the empty page is 78 px shorter, because VH-187 and VH-196 lifted the file button.

What does not hold is narrow: one misleading notice, two placement and focus defects, two small focus rings, and, at rank 3, a sideways scroll at a large font on a phone width.

- **D-05, rank 1.** A kept part with real sound under one 400 ms block is told "This video has no audio", and the new video leaves that sound out. The notice comes before Create, so the loss is not silent, but its stated cause is false.
- **D-06, rank 2.** The heavy-limiting warning's "play the new video before you save it" comes after the player and Save, and the announcement speaks only its heading.
- **V-46, regressed, rank 2.** An unreadable file chosen from Video language drops focus to the page (S-149 ×3). VH-191 moved the radios into the step that the read-failure branch withdraws with no hand-on, and that hand-on was VH-178's omission.
- **V-48, rank 2.** At a 32 px default font on a 320 px Malay page, the onset "?"'s ring is 460 of 464 px² at 3:1.
- **V-49, rank 2.** In the forced palettes, the wrapped "Creators team" link's ring crosses the next link's text (×8).
- **V-18, incomplete, rank 2.** The first action now sits inside the first screen at 1280 × 900 in English and Chinese. In Malay it crosses the fold (890–934 px). At 320 × 568 it is below the first screen in all three languages.
- **V-50, rank 3.** At the 32 px default font in a 320 or 401 px window, the Video type and Video language choices, the closing colour and the trim's Set buttons push the page sideways, to 444–528 px (S-113 ×6). No adopted rule asks for it, but the friction is real.

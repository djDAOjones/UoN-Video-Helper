## How to read it

- **Applies.** "applies" or "n-a" with the reason. 69 apply and 17 are n-a,
  as on 23 October. Phase 8 reached three n-a rows, and each holds: 1.2.4
  (the finished player reads a local file, not a stream), 1.4.13 (both
  toggletips open on a press, never on hover or focus, and Escape or a
  click elsewhere closes each) and 3.2.6 (one page; the footer's "Help and
  feedback" group is its own).
- **Verdict.** Met (measured in the run), met-in-source (source only, no
  instrument reads it), gap or n-a. A gap is typed: **defect**, **accepted
  limitation** or **missing evidence**, with its finding ID where it has
  one. V-01 to V-47 are Phase 7's and keep their IDs, a regression too;
  V-48 on are new. D-01 to D-04 are Codex astra's Phase 7 review, D-05 and
  D-06 its blind review of this pass.
- **Evidence, scoped.** The instrument and the records it rests on; each
  instrument's counts are in its `evidence/measurements/digest-<instrument>-2026-10-27.md`
  and each Phase 8 item's row in `evidence/measurements/contract-2026-10-27.md`.
  A verdict covers the run's 3,207 records only: the 12 records of the four
  gaps the owner accepted (S-107 and S-55c in the fallback mode, S-133) and
  the 214 unrun cells of `PLANS.matrix` (wish VH-172.1) are missing
  evidence, and no verdict extends to them
  (`evidence/measurements/matrix-2026-10-27.md`). "c-d" is the instruments'
  "cannot determine", never a pass. The finish+1s player row, all 823 rows,
  counts as neither pass nor fail until VH-203's after-run (wish VH-199.1).
  The format "?" changed after the run (VH-205): its rows say "manually
  checked in VH-205", "to be measured in VH-203's after-run" or "missing".
- **Unresolved.** What the evidence leaves open. **P** is a person's check,
  on the [checklist](person-checklist-2026-10-27.md). Wish VH-200.1 is the
  walker's readings for VH-203's after-run: the radio's disc, a wrapped
  link's fragments and the background behind each player ring.
  "(blind review: …)" is Codex astra's
  verdict where it differs from this table's
  ([`codex-astra-blind-review-2026-10-25.md`](codex-astra-blind-review-2026-10-25.md),
  from line 178). It read source only, before the run, so its S agrees with
  met or met-in-source, S + E with met-in-source short of a run, E with
  missing evidence, D with a defect and L with an accepted limitation.
- **23 Oct.** Phase 7's verdict in
  [`wcag-2026-10-23.md`](../../2026-10-22/design-pass-2/wcag-2026-10-23.md),
  on VH-172's run. A **mixed** one is not inherited as a pass, and a gap

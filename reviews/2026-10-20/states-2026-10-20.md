what is on screen beyond the frame, what a first-time member of staff is
meant to do, what competes with that, what repeats something in view, and
what should change. States 01–29 keep VH-124's numbers; 30 onward are the
surfaces added since 1 October and the states the critique said were
missing. Each row names a capture in the walker's output; the ones a finding
cites are in `evidence/`.

machine-translation notice on the Chinese and Malay pages), the title, the
lede, Video type, Video language, then the steps and the footer. The empty

to 3,722 px (2,765), a title card to 4,128, the open statement to 5,977; at

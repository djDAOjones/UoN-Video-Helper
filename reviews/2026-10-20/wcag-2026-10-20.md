# WCAG 2.2, criterion by criterion — 20 October 2026

The 86 criteria of WCAG 2.2, judged this time against the live page at
`d38ee79`, in headless Chrome 155 driven as [`README.md`](README.md)
describes, in English, Simplified Chinese and Bahasa Malaysia, on both
sides of the video-type switch, at 1280 px, 390 px, 320 px and 200% zoom,
in the light and dark themes, under the text-spacing overrides and in
emulated forced colours. Codex astra judged the same 86 from source
([`codex-astra-audit-2026-10-20.md`](codex-astra-audit-2026-10-20.md) §5);
where the two differ, the live finding stands and the row says so.

**Verdicts.** **met** — seen on the live page, with the evidence named.
**met-in-source** — the browser could not reach it; source establishes it.
**gap** — a defect (named as a V- finding), a conditional media limitation,
or evidence only a person can supply. **n-a** — the criterion's trigger is
absent. An accepted exception is a recorded limitation, never a pass
(spec §9.3).

## What spec §9.3 should record

- **The preview's content** (1.2.1–1.2.8, 2.3.1–2.3.2): already recorded. Keep; 1.2.4 and 1.2.9 are not applicable and need no entry.
- **kB–TB, UoN and the codec names** (3.1.4): already recorded with their four fields; keep.
- **2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide**: decide and record whether the moving stage, bar and time-left lines are essential to the job's progress (the reading VH-109 implied), or give them a control. One entry either way.
- **The open A and AA gaps while they stand**: 3.3.2 (V-06), 3.1.2 (V-07), 4.1.3 (V-10) — each with its impact and its planned fix, until Phase 3 closes them; then removed.
- **Evidence a person must supply**, as limitations, not failures: the Chinese 40-glyph measure (1.4.8), the translated terms and reading level (3.1.3, 3.1.5), real screen readers (2.4.3, 4.1.3 as heard), real Windows contrast themes (1.4.11, 2.4.13), real phones (1.3.4, 2.5.6).
- **WCAG's own exemptions**, noted so they are not mistaken for exceptions: the native player's controls (2.5.5), the logotype (1.4.9) — already there.

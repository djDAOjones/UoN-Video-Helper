# WCAG 2.2, criterion by criterion — 20 October 2026

The 86 criteria of WCAG 2.2, judged this time against the live page at
`d38ee79`, in headless Chrome 155 driven as [`README.md`](README.md)
describes, in English, Simplified Chinese and Bahasa Malaysia, on both
sides of the video-type switch, at 1280 px, 390 px, 320 px and a 640 px
stand-in for 200% zoom, in the light and dark themes, under the
text-spacing overrides and in emulated forced colours. Codex astra judged
the same 86 from source ([`codex-astra-audit-2026-10-20.md`](codex-astra-audit-2026-10-20.md)
§5) and then argued with this table; where the two differ, the row says so.

**Verdicts.** **met** — seen on the live page, with the evidence named; the
measurements are in [`evidence/measurements/`](evidence/measurements/).
**met-in-source** — the browser could not reach it; source establishes it.
**gap** — a defect (named as a V- finding), a conditional media limitation,
or evidence only a person or a different method can supply; the row says
which. **n-a** — the criterion's trigger is absent. An accepted exception
is a recorded limitation, never a pass (spec §9.3). A live-region change
is a programmatic announcement, not what a screen reader says.

## What spec §9.3 should record

Four kinds, kept apart: WCAG's own exemptions, accepted exceptions (each
with criterion, reason, impact, mitigation and the owner's decision),
defects while they stand, and evidence a person must supply.

- **WCAG's own exemptions**, already noted: the native player's controls
  (2.5.5), the logotype (1.4.9).
- **Accepted exceptions**, already recorded: the preview's content
  (1.2.1–1.2.3, 1.2.5–1.2.8, 2.3.1, 2.3.2 — 1.2.4 and 1.2.9 do not apply and
  need no entry); kB–TB, UoN and the codec names (3.1.4).
- **2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide**: decide whether the moving stage, bar and
  time-left lines are essential to the job's progress, and record the
  reasoning, or give them a control; VH-141 (V-14) carries the decision.
- **Defects while they stand**, with their impact and the item that fixes
  them: 3.3.2 (V-06, VH-140), 3.1.2 (V-07, VH-141), 4.1.3 (V-10, VH-141),
  2.4.3's two hand-offs (V-10, V-12, VH-141); each entry goes when its item
  closes with evidence.
- **Evidence a person or another method must supply**, as limitations, not
  failures: the browser's own 200% enlargement (1.4.4, 1.4.8); the Chinese
  40-glyph measure (1.4.8); the user's own colours beyond the emulation
  (1.4.8); the selects by keyboard (2.1.1); the thumbs' inset ring (2.4.13);
  the translated terms and reading level (3.1.3, 3.1.5); real screen
  readers (2.4.3, 4.1.3 as heard); real Windows contrast themes (1.4.11,
  2.4.13); real phones (1.3.4, 2.5.6); speech input, which §13 wants
  completed or deferred in writing.

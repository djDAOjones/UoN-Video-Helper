## Part A — the findings

1. **V-01: keep rank 1, but identify the defect correctly.** The reported result is plausible from source; its exact durations remain browser evidence I cannot verify here. Inspection reads structure without decoding (`src/media/inspect.ts:180`); `computeDuration()` supplies track durations. Audio analysis consumes available samples, fills gaps before subsequent samples, then flushes at EOF (`src/media/audio-plan.ts:126`, `:158`, `:185`). It does not check that the declared tail arrived.

   Encoding retains those declared spans for the closing timeline (`src/media/pipeline.ts:417`), while audio stops after available samples and the processing flush (`:759`, `:778`). The later closing and constant-frame-rate transform make held picture plausible. Output verification checks loudness and peak, not completeness (`src/media/output-verification.ts:39`); the video check establishes readability, not a complete tail.

   This is a malformed fixture exposing a pipeline completeness defect and a misleading page assurance. It does **not** establish that the tool discarded nine seconds which existed in the input. Neither existing warning should necessarily fire: decode damage detects excessive sample amplitude; extended silence needs measured silence exceeding 30 seconds, not absent samples (`src/audio/warnings.ts:79`, `:138`).

   Add these sources. Remove “§13 criterion 2 (the output content)”: that criterion concerns loudness and peak. Always and the house digest’s “Fail loudly on data loss” support the warning. Revise the direction: the three-second probe cannot certify the tail (`src/media/probe.ts:92`). Compare each track against its own expected kept extent, allowing legitimate offsets and different lengths; do not silently shorten the requested range.

   The proposed distinction between typed and pre-filled text introduces hidden interaction history: identical visible cards could pass or fail differently. Delaying the date also contradicts the explicit fill-on-tick instruction. Remove this rank-2 defect and its prescribed fix. Retain an owner-facing design question about date-only cards if useful; Nielsen’s safe-default principle does not settle that question.

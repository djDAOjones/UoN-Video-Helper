# VH-184 — the onset toggletip, measured in Chrome (2026-10-24)

What it checks: with the tip open, Tab and Shift+Tab close it and focus goes
where it goes with the tip closed; Escape closes it and leaves focus on the
"?"; a click on its own text keeps it open, and Escape then returns focus to
the "?"; a click elsewhere closes it; its edge is no longer the
notification's rail and holds 3:1. Instrument:
`design-pass-2/evidence/measurements/walker/tip-184.mjs`, headless Chrome over
CDP, real key and pointer events, `lecture-30s.mp4` chosen and Fade set.

Grid: 3 languages × 3 widths (1280, 375, 320 px) × 4 colour modes (light,
dark, forced-light, forced-dark) = 36 cases. Readings, every case:
`vh184-toggletip-2026-10-24.json`.

## Before (base 8c144ba, en-GB, 1280 px, light, dark, forced-light)

All three failed: Tab and Shift+Tab left the tip open (focus on the Blue
colour radio and on Animation onset). Its edge was a 4 px left rail in
`--border-strong`, none on the other sides: the notification's form
(`vh184-before-light-tip.png`).

## After: 36 of 36

| Check | Every case |
| --- | --- |
| Tab, tip closed → | the Blue colour radio |
| Tab, tip open → | the Blue colour radio, tip closed |
| Shift+Tab, tip closed → | Animation onset |
| Shift+Tab, tip open → | Animation onset, tip closed |
| Escape | tip closed, focus on the "?" |
| Click on its text | stays open; Escape then closes it, focus on the "?" |
| Click on the Colour legend | closes |
| Edge | 1 px solid on all four sides, `--border-subtle` |

| Mode | Edge colour | vs the tip's ground | vs the step's ground |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| light | #707d89 (uon-blue-60) | 4.08:1 | 3.91:1 |
| dark | #9fa8b1 (uon-blue-40) | 4.71:1 | 5.53:1 |
| forced-light | system black | 21:1 | 21:1 |
| forced-dark | system white | 21:1 | 21:1 |

Captures: `vh184-after-light-tip.png`, `vh184-after-forced-dark-tip.png`.

## What a screen reader hears: unchanged

The tip's markup, its `aria-live="polite"` wrapper, the button's name and
`aria-expanded` are untouched; only when it closes changed. No new element,
control or token: `--border-subtle` is the existing neutral edge.

## Not measured

A person on a real screen reader was not asked. Touch at 375 px was driven as
touch events by the walker's click; a real phone was not used.

## The flows

At 1280 px in light, per language, three flows: **9 of 9 pass**.

- **keep:**
  - The player plays, muted so that a scripted press is not refused; `currentTime` advances by about 0.76 s in 0.8 s. It is left playing.
  - A page-language change keeps the same element, still playing, and renames it in the new language.
  - Create shows the discard question: the player is off the page, and taking it off paused it. "Keep it" puts back the same element and the same URL, with focus on the card.
  - Choosing another file (`silent-10s.mp4`) leaves the same player under "Previous video — …".
  - Create, then Discard: every object URL is counted, and the old one is revoked exactly once, not before. It no longer fetches, and the old element has no `src`. The next video gets its own player and its own URL.
- **save:** each chunk of the save is slowed by 1.5 s, and focus is put on the player while it runs. When the save completes:
  - no player is left in the card, and its URL no longer fetches;
  - focus is on the card;
  - the status reads "Saved." and the lifetime line has gone.
- **download:** with no save dialog the page downloads.
  - The player stays, with the same URL, which still fetches, and the lifetime line reads `result.lifetimeDelivered`.
  - Create, then Discard, revokes the URL.

Traced in source only, and left to pass 3's instruments as wish line VH-192.2: a failed playback has no listener and touches nothing else; a cancelled or failed save keeps the player, since the result stays unsaved; Tab reaches its native controls; a closed tab ends the document's object URLs, and a back-forward restore keeps them.

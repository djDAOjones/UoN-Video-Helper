# VH-169 — a pressed state for each button kind, measured (2026-10-22)

Headless Chrome 155, Vite dev build, 1280 × 900, a 6 s test clip. Each press is a real CDP mouse press (`Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mousePressed, no release), read with `getComputedStyle` while held; the release is moved off the button, so nothing is clicked. Nine kinds, light, dark and forced colours, in en-GB, zh-Hans and ms-MY: 81 presses before and 81 after. `:active` matched on every press both times.

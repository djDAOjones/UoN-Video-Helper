# The second design pass — the plan, 22 October 2026

The owner asked: "lets design and run another full design / ux /
accessibility pass. thorough planning and ticketed before execution".
This is the plan for that pass, PLAN-7, with Codex astra as a full
partner at every stage. In the last pass (VH-163) Codex wrote a blind
review and critiqued the report, but checked none of the six fixes,
VH-164 to VH-169. Codex critiqued a draft of this plan
([`codex-astra-plan-critique-2026-10-22.md`](codex-astra-plan-critique-2026-10-22.md));
what changed is under "After Codex astra's critique". Each stage is an
item, VH-170 to VH-177, whose file carries its acceptance criteria; this
document carries the method they share.

## The rule that holds throughout

The app stays simple: consolidate; no new control, checkbox or element;
the progress bar stays. The rule constrains remedies, never the
report: an unmet criterion is reported truthfully, and a remedy that
would need a new element goes to the owner flagged and recommended
against, as VH-168 did.

## Scope

The whole page, judged as one design and one journey.

### Journeys

Four end-to-end journeys, each judged as a whole for action prominence,
decision burden, reading order, repetition and continuity between steps:

- **J1** — a staff video, the default path: choose, read, title card,
  trim, closing, output, create, save.
- **J2** — a student submission: the card's fields, a refusal, the
  filled card, Internal use only.
- **J3** — recovery: a failure, its next step, a retry.
- **J4** — a second video with the first result kept: saving the
  previous result while the next is checked, an unreadable replacement
  beside a kept result.

### Scenarios

The 64 states become scenarios with IDs (S-01 onward), each with its
setup, the expected transition, the resulting focus and status, and the
values kept. Each catalogue row maps to one or more IDs; a row holding
several behaviours (row 53's save outcomes, row 54's feedback outcomes)
becomes several. Each is walked or marked source-only with the reason;
none stays source-only by default. Equivalent states are reused with
the reason recorded, so there is no full cross-product. Beyond the
catalogue, at least:

- startup pending and blocked; preview unavailable; Video properties
  and the onset help open;
- the pre-flight warnings: captions lost, a cut-short file, quiet and
  loud audio, a long job, 4:3, no audio, too short;
- the previous result's warnings, saving the previous video while
  checking the next, discard;
- save stopped, cancelled or failed, refusal to overwrite the source,
  the download hand-off and retry;
- the feedback dialog empty, filled, oversized and refused, a copy that
  fails, errors captured and Report this problem;
- Video type and Video language switched while running and beside a
  kept result, apart from Page language, and a remembered language;
- focus left where the person is reading when an operation finishes;
- the system check, the accessibility statement and the footer.

### Languages, views, modes and input

- **Languages:** English, Simplified Chinese, Bahasa Malaysia.
- **Widths:** 1280 × 900; 375 × 812 with mobile emulation; 320 × 568;
  a targeted landscape and short-height view.
- **Enlargement, four records kept apart:** narrow-layout reflow
  (320 px); Chrome's own page zoom at 200% and 400% where the
  instrument can set it, else unresolved and on the person list;
  default-font enlargement (Chrome's default font size at 32 px, a
  font-preference test, since type and the 28rem measure follow the
  root size); a synthetic text-only enlargement only if it is shown to
  double text without enlarging the geometry around it. Each records
  the actual viewport and checks content and function, dialogs
  included, not the document's width alone.
- **Modes:** light, dark, emulated forced colours in a light and a dark
  palette, 1.4.12 text spacing, reduced motion, and a font-fallback run
  with the brand faces withheld.
- **Input:** keyboard alone, every journey, in three languages at 1280
  px and in English at 320 px.

The light matrix covers every walked scenario in three languages at
1280, 375 and 320 px. Dark, forced colours, enlargement, spacing,
fallback and landscape cover a finite list fixed in VH-171: empty,
ready with warnings, an open disclosure, an invalid submission,
running, finished, a previous result, discard, a failure, the dialog
and the statement.

## The instruments

The walker is copied from HEAD into this bundle's
`evidence/measurements/walker/`, its source revision named; the old
bundle stays untouched. Before the matrix runs, the instruments are
qualified (VH-171):

- **No silent passes.** The expected scenarios are declared; each run
  asserts the language, mode and state it reached; a fresh run
  directory; missing, duplicate, stale or failed records fail the run.
  Each instrument has a "cannot determine" outcome.
- **Proved to detect.** Temporary failing fixtures in the scratchpad,
  never in `src/`: an overflow, a focus ring too small, a control with
  no accessible name. Each instrument must catch its own.
- **The manifest.** Source commit, instrument revision, browser and OS,
  viewport, scaling method, fixture identity, expected and observed
  coverage, and what stayed unresolved; small supporting measurements,
  not only totals.
- **The accessibility tree** from Chrome's own Accessibility domain:
  names, descriptions, values and states, visually hidden content and
  the dialog's background, beside the DOM. A live-region change is
  logged as a DOM change and as an exposure, never as what a screen
  reader says.
- **Keyboard verification apart from setup.** Setup may click or set
  values; the keyboard route may not. It uses keys only, forward and
  back, through the native selects and player, the dialog's entry and
  exit, and focus after cancel, refusal, completion and retry. A native
  interaction it cannot drive stays unverified.
- **Rendered focus and targets.** Focused against unfocused pixels: the
  area whose pixels change by at least 3:1, against a 2 px perimeter
  reference, including both trim thumbs at their ends and near
  overlap, a chosen segment, the native file button, the blue band and
  both forced palettes; obscuring judged apart. Targets from the actual
  clickable area, wrapped labels included, each exception argued.
  Contrast beyond text nodes: input values, placeholders, the native
  button's text and meaningful graphics.

## The lenses

1. **Visual design, Carbon productive.** Hierarchy (one primary per
   state, the step heading leading), rhythm (one gap per relation),
   alignment, density (page height per state against 20 October and
   the pass's starting build, as context, not a target), every text
   element on one of the eight roles (code included), every block of
   running text within the 28rem measure, one look per component kind
   in every state, and dark and forced colours as designed states.
2. **UX, Nielsen's ten.** One row per heuristic with a verdict and
   evidence across the four journeys; the conveyor's flow (what blocks
   Create, the one next action in each state, the cost of a second
   video); prevention and recovery for each failure and warning; the
   wording against UI-STANDARDS, one name per thing across the page and
   the three languages (parity, not fluency); the catalogue's "Meant
   to do", "Competes" and "Repeats" kept for every scenario.
3. **WCAG 2.2 AAA.** The 86 criteria, each with its applicability, its
   scoped evidence, its verdict (met, met-in-source, gap, n-a) and any
   unresolved part; a gap typed as a defect, an accepted limitation or
   missing evidence. A mixed 20 October verdict is not inherited as a
   pass. All five parts of 1.4.8, the user's colours and enlargement
   included; raw rendered-line counts kept for mixed Chinese and Latin
   lines, with the reading stated (VH-167's half-unit count is not
   assumed). 1.4.13 judged for the toggletip's dismissal. The reading
   level is supporting evidence, not a judgement of comprehension. The
   progress bar's essential-content reading (spec §9) stands.
4. **The six fixes verified.** Each against the contract below, with a
   verdict: holds, partial, regressed or unverified.

"Blind" means independent of Claude's findings in this pass, not of the
history: the snapshot holds the earlier passes and the fix commits,

until the blind review is saved.

**Disagreements.** One of fact is settled by evidence, live or in
source, with both views kept; if evidence cannot settle it, it is
recorded "unresolved". One of judgement (whether it is a defect, its
rank, its remedy) is kept with both positions under "For the owner" in
the report and put in VH-175's ask. The owner decides what remains, and
only the contested remedy waits; approved work goes on. A fix check
that fails is repaired and re-checked, with the follow-up verdict kept,
or, if Claude disagrees, the item stays open and the point goes to the
owner. The `Checked:` line states Codex's verdict as given.

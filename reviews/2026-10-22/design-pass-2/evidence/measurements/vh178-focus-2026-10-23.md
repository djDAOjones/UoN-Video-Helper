# Focus passes on when finished work takes its control away — VH-178, 23 October 2026

V-46 (WCAG 2.4.3), measured before and after with the pass's walker: `walker/transitions.mjs` walks `TRANSITIONS` in `walker/scenarios.mjs`, Chrome 155.0.8059.85 headless, Vite serving the working tree, 1280 × 900. The before is base `392077f`; the after is the candidate diff on it. Each record reaches the moment by setup, places focus by keys (Tab and Shift+Tab, a key event first), lets the transition happen and reads `document.activeElement` at once, before any instrument moves it; then the focus instrument (`qualify.mjs` `focusArea`: the area changing by 3:1 against a 2 px perimeter, 2.4.13) reads the ring on the control reached. A record key is `transition__language__mode`.

A record passes only when focus landed where it must, every condition its transition declares in `asserts` held (focus placed on the control the transition takes away; the check still running at that moment; the save still running when the check settled; the reader's move or the dialogue's opening before the job's stop or the save settled; the fault seen at work), and focus entered nothing on the way but where it ended. At the same moment the walker reads where the focused control stands, before anything scrolls: inside the window or not, and how many of five points in it (centre and corners, 2 px in) another element covers (2.4.11, 2.4.12). Both were added on Codex astra's check (`../../codex-astra-vh178-check-2026-10-23.md`, points 1 and 3); the after-run below is the one taken with them.

Runs, in the session's scratchpad: before `runs/before/transitions-2026-10-23T20-18-29-672Z`; after, with the asserts, `runs/after2/transitions-2026-10-23T21-04-57-276Z` (the twelve) and `…T21-14-10-581Z` (the 36 cells); the routes `runs/routes/priority-part-2026-10-23T20-49-33-126Z-b0d9b0` and J3 again after the worker-prelude change, `runs/routes2/priority-part-2026-10-23T21-26-22-077Z-8d38c9`. ## How each case is reached

- **F14** (new in `faults.mjs`) holds the worker's `preflighted` reply 8 s, its own prelude on the worker's module. Unheld, `prores-1080p.mov` blocks about 20 ms after its check starts and a short file is ready in about a second (probed today), too soon for keys to reach Start time. The verdict is unchanged; the page's deadline is 180 s. Used by T-46.2 and (e). Every prelude on the worker now rides one route, joined in the order set, so F3 and F14 compose; the first route to match took the module alone before (Codex's point 2). `node faults.mjs check F3 F9 F10 F12` passes 4 of 4 after the change, and J3, which sets and releases F3 and F12, passes 37 of 37 steps.
- **F4b at 3 s a chunk** (F4b's new `ms` option): the 60 s video's save takes about 40 s, so it is still running when the hour-long still's check settles (about 27 s). T-46.3 asserts both.
- The native file chooser is the browser's own (VH-168): the walker Tabs to it, then sets the file.
- T-46b-cancelled presses Cancel with its own `click()`, which moves no focus, as a mouse click does in Safari; keys cannot press Cancel without focusing it.

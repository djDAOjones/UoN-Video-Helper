## The change

- **V-40, size.** `.file-input` keeps `--focus-room` (`--focus-width` + `--focus-offset`, 4 px, a new token in `tokens.carbon.css`) as padding round its button, inside its own box, and takes it back with an equal negative margin; its width is `calc(100% + 2 * var(--focus-room))`. The drop zone's 16 px padding holds the 4 px.
- **V-40, colour.** Under forced colours, `.file-input:focus-visible::file-selector-button` takes `CanvasText`, both looks, held or not.
- **V-41.** The handles' disc stops come from `--thumb-disc` (12 px) and `--thumb-halo` (2 px), new beside `--target-min`, in the WebKit, Firefox and disabled thumbs and the three forced-colour thumbs, where they were `--spacing-04` and `--spacing-01` in rem.
- **V-42.** Under forced colours the band's ring is drawn inside the focused face, inside its border: `outline-width` the ring's 2 px plus a border, offset inward by the ring and two borders, in `ButtonText`, `HighlightText` on the chosen face. Outside forced colours it is unchanged.
- **V-33.** `#feedback-message` joins the card fields' `max-width: var(--measure)` list: a cap, not a width.
- **V-44.** Under forced colours, a filled or danger button and the unchosen chooser take `border-color: HighlightText` on hover, before the press rule, which still wins.

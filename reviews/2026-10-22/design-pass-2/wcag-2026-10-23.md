# WCAG 2.2 AAA, judged — VH-173, 23 October 2026

The 86 success criteria of WCAG 2.2 at A, AA and AAA, each judged from
VH-172's evidence run (699 records on `76994b8`, whose `src/` is the pass's
starting build, `eae49ea`) and VH-173's live spot checks. The reasoning is
in [`judged-2026-10-23.md`](judged-2026-10-23.md); the findings V-nn are
there too. This is evidence, not a conformance claim.

## How to read it

- **Applies.** "applies" or "n-a" with the reason. 69 apply and 17 are n-a.
  The two the scaffold left to confirm hold: 1.3.5 (the card's fields
  describe a video, not the person filling them in) and 1.4.13 (the
  toggletip opens on a press, never on hover or focus, and Escape or a
  click outside closes it).
- **Verdict.** Met (measured in the run or live), met-in-source (source
  only, no instrument reads it), gap or n-a. A gap is typed: **defect**,
  **accepted limitation** or **missing evidence**, with its V-nn where it
  has one. D-nn is Codex astra's blind review, merged in VH-174.
- **Evidence, scoped.** The instrument and the records it rests on. A
  verdict covers the run's 699 records only; the 821 unrun cells of
  `PLANS.matrix` are missing evidence and no verdict extends to them
  (`evidence/measurements/matrix-2026-10-23.md`). "c-d" is the
  instruments' "cannot determine", never a pass.
- **Unresolved.** What the evidence leaves open. **P** is a person's
  check, on the [checklist](person-checklist-2026-10-23.md).
- **20 Oct.** The verdict of [`wcag-2026-10-20.md`](../../2026-10-20/wcag-2026-10-20.md)
  against `d38ee79`. A **mixed** one is not inherited as a pass, and a gap
  fixed since rests on this pass's evidence, not on the fix's close.

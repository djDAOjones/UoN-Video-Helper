# VH-25 — Boundary fades

Working detail for the open backlog item. Measured 2026-08-25 across the 21
corpus sources.

## What the corpus shows

First and last frame mean luma (0–255), and audio level over the first 300 ms
and last 400 ms:

| Boundary | Needs a fade | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Picture, end (into graphics) | **21 of 21** | always |
| Picture, start | 19 of 21 | nearly always |
| Audio, start | 4 of 19 with audio | sometimes |
| Audio, end | **0 of 19** | never |

The asymmetry is the useful part: **the audio has already stopped by the end,
but the picture never has.** Every source in the corpus ends on a bright frame
— many of the "Module in a Minute" family land on a common endcard at luma
≈83 — so cutting straight into the logo is always a hard visual cut. Meanwhile
no source ends above −69 dBFS, so an audio fade-out at the same boundary is
very nearly a no-op.

The four abrupt audio starts are `Philosophy of Psychotherapy` (−24.9 dBFS),
`AMCS3068` (−26.0), `LIBA2002` (−33.8) and `Pelvis Sculpting` (−34.0) — these
begin mid-speech.

## Detection

Both signals are cheap and already available:

- **Picture**: mean luma of the first and last frame. Threshold ≈24/255 sorts
  the corpus correctly — the only two dark starts (`LIBA3005` at 16,
  `River Evolution` at 17.7) fall below it and everything else is above 40.
- **Audio**: level over the first/last 300 ms, threshold ≈−45 dBFS. The
  loudness DSP already produces momentary values at 10 ms, so this is free —
  no extra decode pass.

## The modal is the design risk

A notice that fires every time is not a notice. The picture fade-out triggers
on 21 of 21 sources, so prompting for it would be pure friction, and staff
would learn to dismiss the dialog without reading — which then also loses them
the cases that matter.

Proposed split:

- **Picture fade-out before graphics: default ON for `hard-cut`, silent.**
  Near-universal, benign, and what the hard cut into the logo needs anyway.
  Visible as a toggle; no dialog. **Not** default-on for the two overlay
  modes — see below.
- **Picture fade-in at start: default OFF.** Nearly always *detectable*, but
  whether a lecture should fade up is editorial, not technical. Offer it.
- **Audio fade-in: the modal case.** Only 4 of 19, and it means the recording
  starts mid-word — genuinely surprising, worth telling someone about, and
  rare enough that the dialog keeps its meaning.
- **Audio fade-out: fold into the picture fade-out.** Never independently
  needed; applying it with the picture costs nothing and avoids a fourth
  control.

Fade length is a config number, not a constant in code (`src/config/`), and
D3's existing 100 ms boundary fade should be reconciled with whatever is
chosen here rather than sitting alongside it.

## Mode coupling (inherited from VH-22 on its close, 2026-08-25)

VH-22 shipped all three boundary modes; the one clause of its acceptance it
could not satisfy was this fade default, which is this ticket's work. Carried
over verbatim in substance:

The maintainer specified no extra fades on the two overlay modes, which matches
what this ticket measured. In `over-picture` and `over-freeze` the 1.00 s build
IS the transition, so a picture fade-out underneath it would double up — the
picture would dip to black while the logo is still arriving over it. Hard cut is
the opposite case: it cuts from a bright frame straight to a finished card, and
21 of 21 corpus sources end bright.

So the picture fade-out defaults **ON for `hard-cut`** and **OFF for the other
two**, rather than globally. It stays available in all three, because a source
that ends on a flash may want it regardless of mode.

# VH-90 — Closing branding: type, onset, colour

> **Status:** Band 4. Working detail only; the decision rationale goes to
> `decision-log.md` when the item ships.

## Intent

Maintainer request, 2026-09-30. The colour choice present at all times, as a
toggle button. Dropdowns for "Animation type" — Cut, Fade, Slide, None — and
"Animation onset" — Over existing, Over generated freeze frame — with a "?"
that explains existing and freeze frame on hover or click. Type first, onset
next, then colour. Onset greyed under Cut and None; colour greyed under None.

## Done when

See the backlog line. Defaults unchanged: Cut, blue.

## Evidence / context

Today (`index.html`, VH-46b): one four-way radio — Clean cut, Over the
picture, Over a freeze frame, No closing sequence — each with a sentence
saying what it does to the last second and how many seconds it adds.
Animation and Colour sit in a "Closing sequence options" disclosure, and
Animation is hidden unless the mode plays the build.

The pipeline takes `BrandingChoice { closing, mode, style, colour }`
(`src/config/branding.ts`) and does not change:

| Type | Onset | `closing` | `mode` | `style` | Adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Cut | disabled | true | `hard-cut` | unread | 4 s |
| Fade | Over existing | true | `over-picture` | `fade` | 4 s |
| Fade | Over generated freeze frame | true | `over-freeze` | `fade` | 5 s |
| Slide | Over existing | true | `over-picture` | `slide` | 4 s |
| Slide | Over generated freeze frame | true | `over-freeze` | `slide` | 5 s |
| None | disabled | false | unread | unread | 0 s |

Eleven distinct jobs once colour is counted: two for Cut, eight for Fade and
Slide, one for None.

## Approach

- **The mapping** is one pure function beside `BrandingChoice`, tested over
  every row and both colours, plus an unrecognised DOM value falling back to
  the default as `chosenBranding` does now.
- **Dropdowns:** native `<select>`, which is what Carbon's Select is.
- **The "?":** Carbon's toggletip, not its tooltip — a button that discloses
  text. Click, Enter and Space open it; Escape or a click elsewhere closes
  it; focus stays on the button. It may ALSO open on hover, but only with
  WCAG 1.4.13 met — dismissible, hoverable, persistent. A 44 px target named
  "About animation onset".
- **Colour:** a two-option radio group styled as a segmented switch, each
  half showing a swatch and the word. Native radios bring the arrow keys and
  the state with them; Carbon's content switcher is a tablist, the wrong role
  for a form value. Swatches use the brand tokens.
- **Disabled:** the native attribute, plus a visible reason beneath ("Not
  used with Cut"). A disabled control leaves the tab order and cannot explain
  itself.
- **The result line,** polite, updated on every change:
  - "Your video cuts to the blue closing card. Adds 4 seconds."
  - "The closing fades in over your last second of video. Adds 4 seconds."
  - "Your last frame is held while the closing slides in. Adds 5 seconds."
  - "No University closing will be added."
- All three controls stay inside one `<fieldset>`, so the job lock in
  `applyControlLock` covers them without new code.

Draft text for the "?": "Over existing — the closing animation plays over the
last second of your video, covering it as it builds. Over generated freeze
frame — your last frame is held for one extra second and the animation plays
over that, so nothing in your video is covered."

## Constraints

- Sentence case; visible labels match accessible names.
- VH-78 (show the closing card being chosen, Icebox) would attach to the
  colour switch. This item does not build it.
- Doc-deltas on ship: spec §4.1 and §4.3 describe the boundary mode as one
  choice.

## Open questions

- Which onset is selected when Fade or Slide is first chosen. Default: Over
  existing, first in the list as asked. The freeze frame is the one that
  covers nothing.
- Whether "None" says what it means in the option itself — "None — no
  closing". Default: "None" as asked, with the result line doing the work.

# VH-12 — Real branding assets

Working detail for the open backlog item. Measured 2026-08-25 from the five
`.mov` files in `samples/` (gitignored).

## What was delivered

| File | Codec | Pixel format | Size | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UoN Closing Logo Fade Blue 2025` | qtrle | argb | 78.9 MB | 5.000 s |
| `UoN Closing Logo Fade White 2025` | qtrle | argb | 80.0 MB | 5.000 s |
| `UoN Closing Logo Slide Blue 2025` | qtrle | argb | 79.4 MB | 5.000 s |
| `UoN Closing Logo Slide White 2025` | qtrle | argb | 80.5 MB | 5.000 s |
| `UoN Logo Exit Animation JB 2023` | qtrle | argb | 78.9 MB | 5.000 s |

All five: 3840×2160, 25 fps, 125 frames, no audio track.

## The four blockers

**1. WebCodecs cannot decode `qtrle`.** It is not a registered WebCodecs codec,
and Mediabunny cannot help — its video support is avc/hevc/vp8/vp9/av1/prores.
The browser cannot open these files at all. They need a build-time transcode to
a format carrying alpha that decodes natively. VP9-with-alpha in WebM is the
realistic target: Chrome decodes it, alpha rides as a separate plane, and the
files will be a fraction of 79 MB. Verify decode in each supported browser
before committing — alpha support is thinner than baseline VP9 support.

**2. They are composite assets, not concatenation assets.** Mean alpha measured
per frame (`alphaextract,signalstats`, 48×27):

| t (s) | 0.16 | 0.40 | 0.64 | 0.88 | 1.16 → 4.88 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fade | 16.0 | 75.0 | 154.0 | 233.0 | 255 (opaque) |
| Slide | 17.5 | 89.7 | 179.5 | 244.9 | 255 (opaque) |

The ramp completes at exactly 1.00 s and holds opaque for the remaining 4 s.
Fade is near-linear; Slide eases faster. Blue and White share an identical
matte — the alpha figures are byte-identical, so style is a colour choice over
one of two mattes. The 2023 asset's alpha matches the 2025 Fade exactly.

**3. No audio bed.** Spec §4.4 builds the −16 LUFS mastering rule on the bed
existing and passing through unprocessed. There is no audio track, so the
closing 5 s is silent. This also makes VH-21 (keep the bed on a silent source)
cosmetic on current assets.

**4. One master, not four.** 4K25 only. Spec §4.2 assumed a 1080p/2160p ×
25/30 matrix, so the code picks a matching variant. Against the real corpus
that variant does not exist: branding must scale to 640×480, 852×480,
1280×720, 1920×1080 and 3840×2400, and convert to 30, 30.303 and 50 fps.
Scaling a 4K master down is safe; the frame-rate conversion is the risk, and
25 → 30.303 has no clean cadence.

# VH-12 — Real branding assets

Working detail for the open backlog item. Measured 2026-08-25 from the five
`.mov` files in `samples/` (gitignored).

## What was delivered

| File | Codec | Pixel format | Size | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UoN Closing Logo Fade Blue 2025` | qtrle | argb | 78.9 MB | 5.000 s |
| `UoN Closing Logo Fade White 2025` | qtrle | argb | 80.0 MB | 5.000 s |
| `UoN Closing Logo Slide Blue 2025` | qtrle | argb | 79.4 MB | 5.000 s |
| `UoN Closing Logo Slide White 2025` | qtrle | argb | 80.5 MB | 5.000 s |
| `UoN Logo Exit Animation JB 2023` | qtrle | argb | 78.9 MB | 5.000 s |

All five: 3840×2160, 25 fps, 125 frames, no audio track.

## The four blockers

**1. WebCodecs cannot decode `qtrle`.** Not a registered WebCodecs codec, and
Mediabunny's video support is avc/hevc/vp8/vp9/av1/prores. The browser cannot
open these files at all; they need a build-time transcode. Alpha support is
thinner than baseline codec support, so verify decode in every supported
browser before committing to a format — see the factorisation below, which
cuts the alpha-carrying material to 4 seconds total.

**2. They are composite assets, not concatenation assets.** Mean alpha measured
per frame (`alphaextract,signalstats`, 48×27):

| t (s) | 0.16 | 0.40 | 0.64 | 0.88 | 1.16 → 4.88 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fade | 16.0 | 75.0 | 154.0 | 233.0 | 255 (opaque) |
| Slide | 17.5 | 89.7 | 179.5 | 244.9 | 255 (opaque) |

The ramp completes at exactly 1.00 s and holds opaque for the remaining 4 s.
Fade is near-linear; Slide eases faster. Blue and White share an identical
matte — the alpha figures are byte-identical, so style is a colour choice over
one of two mattes. The 2023 asset's alpha matches the 2025 Fade exactly.

**3. No audio bed — and that is intended.** Spec §4.4 builds the −16 LUFS
mastering rule on a bed that exists and passes through unprocessed. There is no
audio track, and the maintainer confirms silent graphics are "more native"
(2026-08-25). So the rule is struck rather than satisfied, and VH-21 (keep the
bed on a silent source) is moot on these assets.

**4. One master, not four.** 4K25 only. Spec §4.2 assumed a 1080p/2160p ×
25/30 matrix, so the code picks a matching variant. Against the real corpus
that variant does not exist: branding must scale to 640×480, 852×480,
1280×720, 1920×1080 and 3840×2400, and convert to 30, 30.303 and 50 fps.
Scaling a 4K master down is safe; the frame-rate conversion is the risk, and
25 → 30.303 has no clean cadence.

## Two findings that change what we ship (2026-08-25)

**The alpha is PREMULTIPLIED, matted with black.** On the White variant, whose
artwork reaches RGB 255 when opaque, RGB is exactly capped at alpha: t=0.16 s
gives alpha 16 / max RGB 16; t=0.40 s gives 75 / 75; t=2.00 s gives 255 / 255.
So the composite is `out = brand + source × (1 − a)`, NOT
`out = brand × a + source × (1 − a)`. Using the straight-alpha form would
double-darken the logo and leave black fringes on every edge — wrong in a way
that looks plausible, which is the worst kind. Premultiplied is also the
better form for our case: it resamples cleanly, and we must scale 4K down to
640×480. Keep it; just composite correctly.

**The assets factorise, and only 1 second of them needs alpha.** Alpha reaches
255 across the whole frame at exactly t=1.00 s and never drops again (94
frames checked, minimum 255). And frames after t=1.00 s are BYTE-IDENTICAL
between Fade and Slide — the md5 of the decoded frame matches at 1.20, 2.00,
3.00, 4.00 and 4.90 s. Blue and White genuinely differ throughout.

So four 5 s masters are really:

- **2 tails** (Blue, White) — 4 s, fully opaque, no alpha, shared across
  styles. Plain H.264 or VP9, universally decodable, small.
- **4 onsets** (2 styles × 2 colours) — 1 s each, alpha required.

That removes the hard part from most of the asset. Only 4 seconds of
alpha-carrying material ships in total, and "clean cut" mode needs none of it.
The tail is animated throughout, so it cannot collapse to a still.

## Settled by the maintainer, 2026-08-25

- **Work approved** in full.
- **Default: Fade Blue.** All four styles ship; staff can choose. Colour is
  worth exposing because during the onset the graphic appears *over* the
  footage, where white on bright content can read poorly.
- **`UoN Logo Exit Animation JB 2023` is retired.**
- **The shared tail is deliberate.** The maintainer authored the assets by
  duplicating one After Effects composition and varying the onset animation and
  colour — "deliberate consistency". So the identical tail is a guaranteed
  property, not an export artefact, and shipping two shared tails is safe.
  Note the precise shape: the tail is identical *within a colour* (Fade Blue =
  Slide Blue after t=1.00 s), while Blue and White differ throughout. Two
  tails, four onsets.

## Spike result, 2026-08-25 — alpha decode WORKS

Mediabunny stores alpha as packet side data and merges colour and alpha on the
CPU (`ColorAlphaMerger`), so it needs only ordinary VP9 decode, not native
browser alpha-video support. Verified end to end rather than reasoned about:
`/spike-alpha.html` (dev-only) loads each generated asset through Mediabunny,
draws it via `CanvasSink({ alpha: true })` and reads back pixel alpha.

| Asset | `canBeTransparent()` | decoded alpha | result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| onset fade blue / white | true | 75–75 | pass |
| onset slide blue / white | true | 0–255 | pass |
| tail blue | false | 255 | pass (opaque as intended) |

The readings cross-check against the masters: Fade is a uniform whole-frame
opacity ramp (75 everywhere at t=0.4 s, exactly the master's value), Slide is a
hard-edged wipe (both extremes present, only the moving edge partial). The
style distinction survives conversion and decode intact.

**Verified on Chromium only** — that is what the in-app browser runs. Safari 26
and Firefox are unverified; the spike page exists so a maintainer can check them
by opening one URL. VP8 + alpha failed the same test and was not pursued: VP9 is
smaller and works. If Safari ever fails VP9 alpha, retry VP8 with a corrected
encode before concluding.

## Assets built, 2026-08-25

`scripts/build-branding.mjs` produces 12 files totalling **0.74 MB** — against
the ~100 MB this ticket originally estimated for eight 20 Mbps masters, and
79 MB per source master. The split is frame-exact: onsets are 25 frames /
1.000 s, tails 100 frames / 4.000 s, and the tail's first frame matches the
master at t=1.00 s to within compression noise (max channel difference 3).
Tails measure PSNR 63.0 dB / SSIM 0.9999 against the masters.

The script refuses to build from a master that is not 3840×2160, 25 fps and
125 frames, because every downstream number — the 1.00 s split, the mode
durations, the shared tail — depends on that shape holding across re-exports.

## Still to do

The app has NOT been rewired yet and still loads the placeholders, so the build
never references a missing file. Remaining:

- `src/config/branding.ts` — `BRANDING_MASTERS` describes a 25/30 frame-rate
  matrix that does not exist, `brandingAssetName` has no notion of style,
  colour or the onset/tail split, and `selectBrandingMaster` matches on a frame
  rate that is now always 25.
- `BRANDING_DURATIONS.closingSeconds` is 4, but the added duration is
  mode-dependent: T+4.00 for hard transition and transition, T+5.00 for
  transition with still frame (VH-22).
- `loadBrandingClip` passes `formats: [new Mp4InputFormat()]` and must accept
  WebM for the onsets.
- The compositor itself, using the premultiplied formula.
- Style/colour choice in the UI, defaulting to Fade Blue.

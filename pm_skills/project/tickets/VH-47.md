# VH-47 — "Best quality" ignores the source bitrate

Found 2026-08-25 reviewing VH-41, which capped the *smaller* preset at the
source and deliberately exempted this one.

## The exemption is right; the formula it exempts is not

VH-41's reasoning stands. Re-encoding at exactly the source bitrate yields
something worse than the source: the decoded frames already carry compression
artefacts — ringing, blocking, mosquito noise — and to the second encoder
those are high-frequency detail it must spend bits preserving. Equal bitrate is
not equal quality on a second generation, and this preset goes to EchoVideo and
YouTube, which re-encode again on ingest. The headroom is real and worth
keeping.

But spec §6.1 sets the figure at `pixelRate × 0.12` — resolution and frame rate
only. It never looks at the source, so the headroom it grants bears no
relationship to what the source actually needs.

## Measured

| Source | `pixelRate × 0.12` asks | Ratio to source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teams 1080p16 @ 1.006 Mbps | 3.98 Mbps | **4.0x** |
| Camera master 1080p30 @ 20 Mbps | 7.46 Mbps | **0.37x** |

The formula gives 4x headroom to the file with nothing left to protect, and
takes 63% of the bitrate away from the pristine master that needed it. It is
inverted with respect to its own stated purpose.

The Teams figure is not hypothetical — it is the corpus's own recording. A
60 MB file through "best quality" becomes roughly 240 MB carrying no more
detail than the 60 MB did.

## What to do

Make the figure source-relative — a band, not a ceiling:

    best = clamp(pixelRate × 0.12, source × 1.2, source × 2.0)

- Teams: 3.98 → **2.01 Mbps**. Still 2x headroom, less than half the file.
- Camera master: 7.46 → **24 Mbps**. Protection it does not get today.

Keep `pixelRate × 0.12` as the fallback when the bitrate cannot be measured.
`outputShapeFor` already accepts `videoBitrateBps?: number | null` and already
declines to guess when it is absent (VH-41); this rides that.

The multipliers are the part to argue about. 1.2 is roughly the floor below
which a second generation starts to show on detailed content; 2.0 is roughly
where further bits go into storing the first encoder's artefacts rather than
the picture. Both are judgement rather than measurement. The calibration probe
could measure them if it is worth the time.

## Sequencing

Same function and the same visit as VH-31 and VH-19, and it should go **first**
of the three: VH-31 re-grounds the *estimate* in real measurement, and the
estimate should be grounded against the bitrate that will actually be
requested. Doing them apart means touching `outputShapeFor` twice and reasoning
about the estimate against a figure about to change.

## Spec

Changes §6.1's bitrate row from a fixed bits-per-pixel figure to a
source-relative band. Recorded in `doc-deltas.md`; `docs/` is protected and
this item does not edit it.

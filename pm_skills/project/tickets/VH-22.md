# VH-22 — Branding boundary modes

Working detail for the open backlog item. Definitions confirmed by the
maintainer 2026-08-25.

## The shape of the asset

The closing graphic is 5.00 s: an **onset** [0.00, 1.00] where alpha ramps
0 → 255, and a **tail** [1.00, 5.00] that is fully opaque. Measured in VH-12.
Write `T` for the source duration.

## The three modes

**1. Hard transition** — the graphic's onset is discarded.

    source [0, T] ++ graphic [1.00, 5.00]

Output `T + 4.00 s`. No compositing at any point, so this is the only mode
reachable without alpha decode. The logo appears already fully formed.

**2. Transition** — the graphic starts *before* the source ends, so its onset
plays over the closing second of video.

    source [0, T-1.00]
    source [T-1.00, T]  composited under  graphic [0.00, 1.00]
    graphic [1.00, 5.00]

Output `T + 4.00 s` — same as mode 1. No source content is cut, but the last
second of it is progressively obscured as the logo arrives.

**3. Transition with still frame** — the graphic starts *when* the source ends,
and the final frame sustains beneath the transparent part.

    source [0, T]
    freeze(frame at T) held 1.00 s  composited under  graphic [0.00, 1.00]
    graphic [1.00, 5.00]

Output `T + 5.00 s`. Nothing is obscured; the cost is a second of frozen
picture.

So modes 1 and 2 produce the same duration and mode 3 is one second longer.
The choice between 2 and 3 is precisely whether the closing second of content
matters — a lecture ending on a dense slide or a "thank you" card wants 3; one
ending on a held wide shot is fine with 2.

## Audio

The graphics are silent by design (VH-12). Source audio runs to `T` in every
mode and the remainder is silence — 4.00 s in modes 1 and 2, 5.00 s in mode 3.
Mode 2 needs no special handling: the audio simply continues under the
compositing second.

## Interaction with VH-25

The maintainer specified "no additional fades" for modes 2 and 3, which is
consistent with what VH-25 measured: in those modes the graphic's own onset
*is* the transition, so a picture fade-out would double up. Mode 1 is the
opposite case — it cuts from a bright frame straight to a fully-formed logo,
and 21 of 21 corpus sources end bright.

So the picture fade-out defaults **ON for mode 1** and **OFF for modes 2 and
3**, rather than defaulting globally. It stays available in all three.

## Open

- Sources shorter than 1.00 s cannot support mode 2. Degrade to mode 3 (which
  always works) rather than erroring, and say so.
- Mode 3 freezes the final decoded frame. On a source whose last frame is
  corrupt or black-flashed — not rare in screen recordings — that flaw is held
  for a full second. Consider taking the freeze from the last *clean* frame.

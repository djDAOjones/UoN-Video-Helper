# VH-22 — Branding boundary modes

Working detail for the open backlog item. Definitions confirmed by the
maintainer 2026-08-25; names are the conventional edit terms.

## The shape of the asset

The closing graphic is 5.00 s: a **build** [0.00, 1.00] where alpha ramps
0 → 255, and a **card** [1.00, 5.00] that is fully opaque (measured, VH-12).
Write `T` for the source duration.

## The three modes

What actually differs is what sits UNDER the build, which is why they are
named for that.

| Mode | Timeline | Output | Composites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **hard cut** | `source[0,T]` + `graphic[1.00,5.00]` | `T + 4.00` | nothing |
| **over picture** | build over `source[T−1.00,T]`, then card | `T + 4.00` | yes |
| **over freeze frame** | build over a held final frame, then card | `T + 5.00` | yes |

**hard cut** is the default: least for a user to think about, and the only mode
reachable without alpha decode, so it is also the fallback if a browser cannot
decode transparency.

**over picture** and **over freeze frame** produce the same result except for
what is underneath. The choice between them is precisely whether the closing
second of content matters — a lecture ending on a dense slide wants the freeze;
one ending on a held wide shot does not.

## Audio

The graphics are silent by design (VH-12). Source audio runs to `T` in every
mode and the remainder is silence — 4.00 s for the first two, 5.00 s for the
freeze. No special handling: the audio simply continues under the build.

## Interaction with VH-25

The maintainer specified no extra fades on the two overlay modes, which matches
what VH-25 measured: there the build IS the transition, so a picture fade-out
would double up. Hard cut is the opposite — it cuts from a bright frame to a
finished card, and 21 of 21 corpus sources end bright.

So the picture fade-out defaults **ON for hard cut** and **OFF for the other
two**, rather than globally. It stays available in all three.

## Implementation notes

- Source and build frames must be paired **by timestamp, not by frame order**.
  The build is 25 fps and sources are not — a 16 fps Teams recording has 16
  frames in the window against the build's 25. `VideoSampleSink.getSample(t)`
  returns the last frame at or before `t`, which is the pairing wanted.
- `compositePremultiplied` does the blend on the CPU. Canvas `drawImage`
  cannot: it treats the decoded colour as straight and double-darkens
  (measured — 202 where 255 is correct). See composite.ts.
- The freeze takes the last CLEAN frame, not the last decoded one (freeze.ts).

## Open

- A source shorter than 1.00 s cannot support **over picture**. Degrade to
  **over freeze frame**, which always works, rather than erroring — and say so.

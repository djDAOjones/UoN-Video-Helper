## Evidence / context

Read 2026-09-30 from the live site and its brand pages (`/brand/visual/` —
colour, logos, fonts). Summarised here, not copied: read the pages again
before building.

**The site.** Header and footer in Nottingham Blue, the white logo top-left
(197 × 72 px at a 1024 px viewport). Body text Nottingham Blue on white. Lora
bold headings; Circular body with Arial behind it. Pill-shaped buttons.

**The palette.** Hex values are the brand's; ratios are this project's
arithmetic, by the same formula as `test/contrast.test.ts`.

| Colour | Hex | Use here | Measured |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nottingham Blue | `#10263B` | text, bands, primary action | 15.4 on white, 14.0 on the 5% tint, 14.3 on Portland Stone |
| Blue 80% | `#405162` | secondary text | 8.2 on white, 7.4 on the 5% tint, 7.6 on Portland Stone |
| Blue 60% | `#707D89` | borders only | 4.2 on white — clears 3:1, fails as text |
| Blue 20% | `#CFD4D8` | dividers, disabled fill | — |
| Blue 5% | `#F3F4F5` | page ground | — |
| Portland Stone | `#FAF6EF` | panel surface | — |
| Portland Stone 40% | `#FDFBF9` | alternative ground | — |

Brand rules that bind: black is out of the palette, so text is Nottingham
Blue or white; pure white is discouraged as a digital ground in favour of
the 5% blue or the 40% Portland Stone; at most two supporting colours, with
Nottingham Blue dominant; AAA preferred, AA accepted.

What the palette cannot do is status. Jubilee Red is 6.4:1 on white. Error,
success and warning keep Carbon's -80 steps, which measure 10.4–10.7 on the
new surfaces.

**Logo.** A protected trademark, not to be altered. On digital the Nottingham
Blue logo is first choice and the white-out version is for where that is
illegible, such as a blue band. Exclusion zone: half the castle square's
depth, all round. Top-left. Heights by viewport width: 50 px to 767, 80 px to
1200, 130 px above; below 45 px the location line comes off. Files (JPEG,
EPS, PNG) are in the brand team's SharePoint library, linked from the logos
page.

**Fonts.** Circular is licensed through the brand team. Lora is OFL. The
brand's own substitutes are Arial for Circular and Georgia for Lora.

## Approach

- `tokens.brand.css` gains the tints and takes over text, ground, surface,
  border and interactive colour. `tokens.carbon.css` keeps spacing, type
  scale, status and the 44 px floor. With `--interactive` Nottingham Blue,
  links are underlined: colour no longer tells them from text.
- A header band with the logo and the app name; a footer band with System
  check (VH-88), feedback (VH-93) and the version. Neither is sticky — a
  sticky band can obscure focus (WCAG 2.4.11).
- The dark theme is kept: Nottingham Blue ground, white text (15.4), the 20%
  tint for secondary text (10.3), the 40% tint for borders (6.4).
- Lora as a self-hosted WOFF2 with Georgia behind it; body in Arial, then
  the system sans.
- Logo and font are imported through the module graph, or inlined. `public/`
  is off in the Xerte build, and `check:build` fails on any folder.

## Constraints

- Shape, spacing and states stay Carbon productive. The site's pill buttons
  are not adopted.
- The repository is public and `package.json` declares MIT. A trademarked
  logo needs a stated carve-out — as do the closing masters already in
  `public/branding/`.
- In Xerte the app fills a same-origin frame with no chrome round it, so its
  header is the only one on screen.

## Open questions

- The logo file, and which: the white-out version from the brand library, or
  the website's own SVG. Default: the maintainer supplies it.
- Its height. The brand table says 80 px at laptop widths — a header of
  about 160 px with the exclusion zone — and the website itself runs 72.
  Default: 50 px, the table's narrowest tier, at every width. The brand team
  arbitrates.
- The ground. Default: the 5% blue tint, with Portland Stone panels.
